## Arithmetic

At 15% body fat with lean held at the lab 59.6 kg, the target weight is 59.6 ÷ 0.85 ≈ **70.1 kg**. From ~75.7 kg that is 5.6 kg: about 1 kg of glycogen water, which comes off in the first two weeks on lower carbohydrate, and about 4.6 kg of tissue. At 7,700 kcal per kg:

| Rate | Deficit per day | Weeks for 4.6 kg | Reaches ~70.1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **0.2 kg/wk (chosen)** | ~220 | 23 | ~Apr 12 |
| 0.25 kg/wk | ~275 | 18 | ~Mar 8 |
| 0.3 kg/wk | ~330 | 15 | ~Feb 15 |

All three sit far under the ~0.5 kg/wk (0.7% of bodyweight) ceiling that protects lean mass.

**Where the 220 kcal comes from.** The Phase 5 tiers held flat at ~31 km a week with no lifting. The new structure prescribes 36–40 km and three lifts, which adds roughly +60–80 kcal/day from the extra running and +130 from the lifts, about +200 in all. The deficit is therefore conditional on delivering the training, which is why the skipped-session re-tier rule matters: a missed session takes its carbs with it, so the deficit holds when delivery slips. **Assumption:** the energy cost estimates are bottom-up, not measured; the rate reads are what test them.

**Rate-read power.** A least-squares slope over three to four weeks with 12–15 readings has a standard error of roughly 0.12–0.15 kg/wk. The −0.4 trigger sits 0.2 from target and can fire on one read. The −0.1 trigger sits only 0.1 from target, so the −100 kcal step needs two consecutive slow reads, and a single slow read sends the coach to the social and snack counts first, which cost nothing to check.

**Tiers.** P170 (680 kcal) and F60 (540 kcal) leave carbs = (tier − 1,220) ÷ 4: Rest 1,850 → C158 · Easy-Strength 1,950 → C183 · Quality 2,250 → C258 · Long 2,450 → C308. Reference week (1 rest, 4 easy-strength, 1 quality, 1 long) = 14,350 ÷ 7 ≈ 2,050. The fallback (−100, the July 2026 tiers) is Rest 1,750 / C133 · 1,850 / C158 · 2,150 / C233 · 2,350 / C283, and it is the floor: one more step would put Rest carbs at ~107 g, rejected on 2026-05-06.

## Rejected alternatives

- **0.25–0.3 kg/wk on the July tiers.** It reaches 70 kg sooner, but costs more hunger and puts more pressure on the lifts and on Dec 12. The athlete asked for "not the cut targets". It stays as the fallback.
- **Build tiers −150 (the "midpoint").** On the August evidence it is roughly maintenance or a slow regain with social evenings, not a cut.
- **An open-ended cut that ends when ~70 kg is reached.** It could run into the spring race window; the race is run at maintenance.
- **A second −100 below the July tiers.** Rest-day carbs would fall to ~107 g, rejected on 2026-05-06.
- **A planned Christmas diet break at the tiers +200.** About 0.2 kg of progress for a psychological gain the exclusion window already gives.
- **A daily treat budget of ~150 kcal inside the tiers**, and **not tracking snacks at all**. The one-word count gives the report what it needs at no cost.
- **Four fixed weigh-in days.** A noisier slope with no slack for a missed morning.
- **Deferring the running review to Oct 25.** It would keep volume capped near 31 km, which removes most of the slow cut's deficit.
