About 8.5 km door to door. Five reps rather than the usual six, because this is the first quality since the infection and Oct 6's overshoot is still on the record.

- **Rep 1 runs no faster than ~5:20/km** and opens at the low end of the band. After that, pace is an output of the heart rate. Judge each rep by its **end-of-rep maximum**, not its average, because the walks lower the entry HR.
- **Ceiling 168.** If a rep's HR passes 168 before 600 m, back off until it drops under 165 and finish the kilometre there. **If the end-of-rep maximum passes 168 on two reps in a row, stop at that rep and jog home easy.** On Oct 6 it passed 168 on three reps in a row and all six were still run, and that overshoot is the likeliest cause of the next morning's resting HR. - **No closing push on rep 5.** If HR will not reach 152 at RPE 7 by rep 2, finish the session at an easy effort and tell me.
