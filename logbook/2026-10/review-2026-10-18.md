# Running review, 2026-10-18

Requested by the athlete on Telegram ("slower, heavier ... let's get to the bottom of this"). A Fable-model agent reviewed `running.md`, `training.md`, `nutrition.md`, the Sep 29 consult and audit, the October daily files and the Garmin history from Feb 1 (141 runs, 91 weigh-ins). The agent's figures are reconstructed from reports and live pulls and have not been independently re-checked by the coach; the prediction history before today in particular comes from earlier reports, because Garmin exposes only the current value.

## Findings

1. **He is slower over the last three weeks, by about 2–3% at threshold.** Threshold km at 167–171 bpm: Sep 11 4:52–4:58 (19 °C) against Oct 9 4:59–5:02 (14 °C, sick). Easy pace at ~141 bpm: ~6:28 on Sep 20 against ~7:00–7:07 on Oct 13, 16 and 18, with heavy confounds (first run after antibiotics, resting HR 57–59, alcohol the night before Oct 18). Confidence medium.
2. **The cut did not make him faster.** The prediction line ran 49:01 (Jan 31) → 47:45 (Mar 26) → 49:48 (Jun 8) → 51:00 (Sep 29) → 51:44 (Oct 18): a near-monotonic slide since April, through the whole cut. Matched 10K-pace reps were identical on Jun 30 and Jul 28.
3. **The fast period was the Garmin-plan block of Feb 2 – Mar 22**: about 37 km/week, a 13–19 km long run every week, long runs at 158–168 avg HR, one quality session, prediction 49:01 → 47:45. The cut averaged 31 km/week with long runs at 138–149. What moved the line whenever it moved the right way was ≥35 km for consecutive weeks plus a long run, not easy-pace discipline.
4. **The calorie increase is not a plausible cause.** Reverse began Sep 21 and the +300 tiers on Oct 5. The same weeks ran 17.5 / 20.6 / 27.2 km, with a holiday, pharyngitis Oct 9–13, no long run since Sep 27, the first loaded squats since Aug 13 on Oct 15, and late onsets. Detraining from low volume plus illness plus an unrecovered week is what the data supports.
5. **Weight: +0.7–1.1 kg on two unconfirmed readings (Oct 7, Oct 12), no confirmed fasted reading in the build.** The "+1.0–1.2 kg is glycogen and water" claim was asserted, not measured. Body water on BIA fell 36.25 → 35.04 kg and scale BF rose 27.3 → 28.6%, so the composition is undetermined. 1 kg is worth about 15–27 s on a 10K, at most half the 44 s prediction move, and his 47:45 came at 76.8 kg.
6. **The band 32–42 km is unreachable under the stack's own rules** (easy run only with a lift, no lift on quality days, three lifts, Saturday rest): about 31 km with 5–6 km easy runs. He was right to run longer; the prescription was wrong.
