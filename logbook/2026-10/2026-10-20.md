### 10K-pace sharpener, ~7.5 km, one Garmin workout

Have the banana and coffee 30 min before, and **600–800 mg of sodium in 500 mL of water** 60–90 min before if you can fit it in (Quality day). Do the Daily 5, then the 2 km warm-up, then the power drill: **3 × 20 m power A-skips** (skip them if calf or Achilles pain is above 2/5).

| Block | Prescription | Governor |
| ----- | ------------ | -------- |
| Warm-up | 2 km easy | HR ≤142 |
| Reps | **3 × 1 km at 10K effort**, **90 s walk** between reps | HR **168–174 by the end of the rep**, RPE 8. Pace guide ~4:55–5:05/km, an output of HR, not a target |
| Cool-down | 2 km easy | HR ≤142 |

- **Rep 1 opens no faster than 5:05/km.** On Oct 14, rep 2 was the fastest kilometre when the instruction was to open low, and 3 of the 5 reps ended over their ceiling. Today the band is higher (10K effort, not sub-threshold), and the same rule applies to it.
- **Ceiling 176.** If a rep's end-of-rep maximum passes 176, that is VO2 work rather than race-pace work. Stop the reps there and jog the cool-down.
- **Three reps, not four, and no closing push.** The point is to touch race effort with the legs fresh for Sunday, not to bank fitness this week. Total work is 3 km, the cap set on Oct 18.
- Run on flat ground. Sunday's climbs are run by effort, and they need no rehearsal.
