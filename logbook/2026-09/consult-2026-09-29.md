**Maintenance estimate (not measured).** Katch-McArdle BMR 1,657 on 59.6 kg lean; ×1.2 ≈ 1,990; + ~350/day from 35–40 km + ~130/day from 3 lifts ≈ **~2,450**. The build band is the instrument that resolves this.

**Cut derivation (Jan 4):** maintenance_verified = build tiers ± any band adjustment that fired; social_term = the average daily surplus implied by the build's weight trend at known plan tiers; cut tier = maintenance_verified − 330 − social_term. **Checkpoint power.** 100 kcal/day ≈ 0.09 kg/wk; SE of a difference of two 7-day fasted means ≈ 0.11 kg → single-week reads cannot decide; hence two consecutive ≥4-reading weeks for the band. Cut-rate reads compare 4-week means: target −1.2 kg/4 wk, act below −0.6 or above −1.6 (effect ≥0.6 kg vs noise ~0.15). Control run: ≥3 bpm across 3 entries. ## Rejected alternatives (with reasons)

- **Race-first (as filed) and "equal" priority.** The athlete retired the A-race; "equal" reproduces the case-by-case tie-breaking under which the pre-lunch double always won and strength went 0/18.
- **Recomp-first now (cut again from Oct 5) and mini-cut to December.** Both repeat the inputs that held weight for seven weeks (deficit, no strength, unlogged social days), and the athlete eats more in the cold months — the stall scenario. Maintenance goes where appetite is high; the deficit goes to Jan–Apr.
- **71 kg / 16% by Jun 1.** Superseded by the athlete's "leaner by August": more runway at the same rate, spring race raced at maintenance.
- **Single 20-week cut Jan–May.** Puts the spring race inside the deficit; no recovery point.
- **+400 build tiers (~2,440 avg).** Athlete preference for a lower plan-day figure as a buffer for 1–2 social days with alcohol. Cost named: less muscle added at a plan-day deficit; the 73.5 floor rule compensates.
- **Bod Pod re-tests (Jan and May).** Declined by the athlete; consequence recorded — 15% is unverifiable by anything owned, so the scorecard's fat channel is the weekly waist tape.
- **Strength auto-downshift (two weeks <2/3 → 2/wk).** Coach recommended it on the evidence (0 of the last 18 planned sessions). Athlete chose flag-only, 3/3 written. Recorded so the next report can hold both statements side by side.
- **3 runs/week.** Athlete raised it to 4–5; floor stays 30 km.
- **Stricter sleep rule (late onset → next day easy).** Rejected in favour of a flag; the same-day <6 h rules already cover the real risk, and `feedback-soft-markers-check-anxiety` says late onset is stress-side, not training-side.
- **Manual / weekly-only workflow.** Both leave the record dependent on the athlete on exactly the days it fails.
