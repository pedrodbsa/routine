Then Aug 17–23: **16.02 km** (holiday plan was ~38 — the Aug 21 easy and Aug 23 long run were lost to gastroenteritis + travel). Aug 24–30: **8.53 km** against a planned ~25 re-entry — and this one is *not* fully explained by illness (see the breach log).

Then: no loaded session after Aug 13 (Aug 18 was bands), and the planned Aug 27 Pull and Aug 28 Legs never happened. The month ends with the **sole surviving lean-mass instrument 18 days dark** (BIA remains uninterpretable under the creatine confound).
4. **Gastroenteritis, trough Aug 22:** RHR 57 (+5), Body Battery peaking at 38, stress avg 36, HRV weekly average 56 — the first LOW-adjacent reading of the record. Fully recovered by the Aug 27 markers (RHR 48, BB 100, HRV 66 and climbing). Handled correctly: no training forced through it, no compensation attempted after it.
