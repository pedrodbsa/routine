## Terrain finding

The athlete asked for cliff-top coastal trail. **That terrain does not exist east of Faro.**
Cliffs are a central and western Algarve feature (Albufeira through Lagos); Tavira sits on the
Ria Formosa, which is salt pan, barrier island and sandbank — flat and fully sun-exposed. The
requested surface was therefore unavailable and the routes were built on the two things that
are actually there: the flat Ecovia do Litoral, and inland asphalt north-west of the town.

Gradient was searched rather than assumed. A 130-point elevation grid (EU-DEM 25 m) over a
5 × 5 km box around Tavira found real relief only in the north-west quadrant, rising to
70–107 m about 3–4 km out. The steepest sustained ramp reachable inside a 7 km run is
**~100 m at 6.1 % on Rua dos Bombeiros Municipais / Rua do Alto do Cano**, 1.5 km from the
bridge, asphalt and lit. The parallel Rua da Bela Fria and Rua das Olarias are cobblestone and
sett — unusable for sprints.

**A — Quatro Águas.** Ponte Romana out along the Estrada das Quatro Águas to the ferry pier and
back. The flattest route in the block at 21 m of gain across 6.5 km, running between the salt
pans with the sea breeze at the far end. No junctions worth the name, so it is the right choice
for the first run in an unfamiliar town.

**B — Ecovia West.** West along the Ecovia do Litoral toward Santa Luzia, turning at 4 km before
the village. Flat, but exposed — there is no shade after the first kilometre, which matters more
here than the gradient does.

**Q — Quatro Águas, Wednesday quality.** The same corridor as A, chosen for the session rather
than inherited: 21 m of gain across 6.5 km, straight, almost no junctions, and a sea breeze at the
pier end. It is the flattest ground in Tavira, which is what a threshold session needs when HR is
the governor and the footing has to be forgettable.

**C — Hill sprints, fallback only.** Superseded as the Wednesday session on 2026-08-13 (see below)
but kept on the watch. A 2.6 km flat warm-up along the river, then the Alto do Cano ramp for
6 × 15 s hill sprints with full walk-back recovery, then the return. The reps are deliberately
left off the course line: repeats do not render usefully as a navigation course, and the ramp is
a single straight climb that needs no route-finding.

**D — Ecovia East.** East along the Ecovia toward Cabanas, turning at 3 km. The shortest run of
the block and it should stay that way — the week's volume is already at the top of its band.

## Wednesday upgraded to a real quality session (2026-08-13)

Athlete-approved, after he asked why the block contained no quality work. The original block gave
a *quality touch* — hill sprints — where the Phase 5 deload rule asks for one *quality session*.
That was a downgrade below spec, folded in under the heat rationale and never argued at the time.
The heat rationale is also weaker than it looked: quality is governed by HR, not pace, and Phase 4–5
has already taken easy pace off the scoreboard, so a high dew point does not invalidate a threshold
session. It only means HR at a given lactate runs high, which is an argument for down-dosing rather
than deleting.

**Session: threshold 3 × 7 min at 165–172 bpm, 2 min walk recovery, WU 1.5 km, CD 1.2 km.**
Garmin workout `1663404450`, scheduled Aug 19.

Ladder derivation at Aug 19: threshold is 15 days old (Aug 4 tempo), sub-T 12 (Aug 7 cruise),
10K-pace 8 (Aug 11 6×1 km). Threshold is both the overdue rung and what `running.md` says the
December 10K needs now — threshold base before speed sharpening. Twenty-one minutes of work against
Jul 21's 24 is a ~13 % cut, which covers the heat rule and the deload without stacking them twice.

**Weekly volume is unchanged at ~35 km.** The upgrade is intensity-only, which is the correct trade:
the deload was always meant to fall on intensity and strength, and strength still drops ~60 % from
having no gym. Walk recovery, not jog, per the standing rule — HR will not fall into a jog band in
two minutes.

Fallback if markers flag on the morning or the 07:00 start is missed: revert to course C and the
hill sprints. That is the Q2 survivability conversion doing what it is for.

Tuesday or Wednesday. Thursday clears Wednesday's session by well over a day and leaves ~72 h before
the Sunday long run. **If only one of the three happens, it should be the plyo block** — it is the
only leg stimulus available for nine days and the part of Leg Day that actually feeds December.
