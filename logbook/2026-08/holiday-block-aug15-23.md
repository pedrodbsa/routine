## Wednesday upgraded to a real quality session (2026-08-13)

Athlete-approved, after he asked why the block contained no quality work. The original block gave
a *quality touch* — hill sprints — where the Phase 5 deload rule asks for one *quality session*.
That was a downgrade below spec, folded in under the heat rationale and never argued at the time.
The heat rationale is also weaker than it looked: quality is governed by HR, not pace, and Phase 4–5
has already taken easy pace off the scoreboard, so a high dew point does not invalidate a threshold
session. It only means HR at a given lactate runs high, which is an argument for down-dosing rather
than deleting.

Ladder derivation at Aug 19: threshold is 15 days old (Aug 4 tempo), sub-T 12 (Aug 7 cruise),
10K-pace 8 (Aug 11 6×1 km). Threshold is both the overdue rung and what `running.md` says the
December 10K needs now — threshold base before speed sharpening. Twenty-one minutes of work against
Jul 21's 24 is a ~13 % cut, which covers the heat rule and the deload without stacking them twice.

**Weekly volume is unchanged at ~35 km.** The upgrade is intensity-only, which is the correct trade:
the deload was always meant to fall on intensity and strength, and strength still drops ~60 % from
having no gym. Walk recovery, not jog, per the standing rule. Fallback if markers flag on the morning
or the 07:00 start is missed: 6 × 15 s hill sprints on any ramp — the Q2 survivability conversion
doing what it is for.

## Strength cut to two sessions, both on non-running days (2026-08-13)

Athlete asked for strength off the running days so the holiday does not turn into two training
slots a day. The non-running days are Sat 15, Tue 18, Thu 20 and Sat 22, and **both Saturdays are
standing rest days**, so there are only two legal slots. The block's three band sessions therefore
become two: **Tue 18 full-upper** (push and pull merged) and **Thu 20 plyo/economy**. Total strength
across the nine days is under an hour.

**Cost, stated plainly:** upper volume drops well below the ~6–9 sets/week/small-muscle retention
target set on 2026-07-31. Over nine days that is acceptable — nine days is too short for meaningful
atrophy, and working loads have been rising, not regressing. It is a real reduction, not a free one.

**If only one session happens, it must be Thursday's plyo block.** It is the only leg stimulus
available for nine days, direct leg loading is already gone for the whole block, and it is the part
of Leg Day that actually feeds the December race. The upper session is retention work and the
cheapest thing on the page to lose.

Spacing checks: Tue upper → Wed quality carries no constraint (the ≥36 h rule governs legs only).
Wed quality AM → Thu plyo PM is ~30 h, and the ≥36 h rule is directional — legs before quality, not
after. Thu plyo → Sun long run is ~72 h. Nothing stacks on a running day.
