## Body Composition & Nutrition — the one real problem

Bodyweight is **flat**. Clean weigh-ins: Jun 3 76.61 → Jun 9 76.43 → Jun 12 76.31 → Jun 17 76.95 kg (Jun 14 and Jun 16 were water/carb-inflated and ignored). The 7-day average sits ~76.5–76.9 across two weeks — a slight gain, not the ~0.26–0.3 kg/wk loss the Phase 4 target calls for. Reference remains the Bod Pod (76.11 kg / 21.7% on Apr 28); scale BIA reads ~7 pp high and is not the governor.

This should not happen on a 45 km week at a planned −325 kcal/day deficit — but **plan-day intake is not the leak.** The athlete **weighs his food and follows the prescribed plan exactly on non-social days** (no digital logging, by design — he executes the weighed plan and reports back, with the 7-day weight trend as the feedback loop). The earlier "tracking is the leak" read is **retracted**. The stall traces to two factors:

- **Social-day surplus (the primary leak).** Jun 4 (beers + churro), 14, 16, 17, and 18 are all above-tier days — well past the one-flex-per-week allowance. One or two alcohol-heavy days can each run +1,500–2,000 over tier, enough to erase the whole week's ~1,950 kcal plan-day deficit on their own. This cluster pulls the weekly average to maintenance.
- **Possible modest TDEE overestimate (secondary).** The ~2,240–2,310 estimate may sit a touch high. Treat as secondary — it can only be isolated after a run of controlled-social-day weeks removes the surplus as the confound.

**Action (this is the month's key decision):** do **not** cut the weighed plan-day meals, and do **not** add hard running (ACWR is already 1.46 — added running volume in a deficit spikes injury/overreaching risk and interferes with quality). The athlete finds eating less hard and prefers adding activity, so build the deficit from three preference-respecting levers: (1) **social-day damage control** — the highest-leverage move (fewer drinks, protein first, skip fried carbs; halve the surplus without skipping the event); (2) **NEAT — daily steps ~8–10k / easy Z2 walking**, the cut's primary deficit lever; and (3) the one small food lever already adopted — **dropping the redundant post-run shake on easy-run days (~−240 kcal)**. Combined ≈ 250–400 kcal/day → restores ~0.25–0.3 kg/wk with the weighed plan meals untouched. Re-read the 7-day trend in ~2 weeks; **a plan-day trim returns only if the scale is still flat on weeks with controlled social days.**
