# DAILY SCHEDULE & WEEKLY PROTOCOLS

## OPTIMAL DAILY TIMELINE

### Standard Daily Schedule (Tuesday-Friday)

**06:30 AM:** Morning run (4-5km, Zone 2, conversational pace 5:30/km)
**07:45-09:00 AM:** Kid care responsibilities
**11:00-12:00 PM:** Upper body strength training (fasted)
**12:30 PM:** Post-workout protein shake + creatine + Vitamin D3/K2
**13:00 PM:** Lunch + B-complex + Omega-3 + Zinc + **Finasteride 1mg**
**17:00 PM:** Afternoon snack + Rhodiola Rosea
**21:00 PM:** Family dinner + Ashwagandha + Astaxanthin
**22:30 PM:** Sleep supplements (Magnesium + L-Theanine + Glycine)
**23:00-23:30 PM:** Target sleep time

### Monday Special Schedule (Padel Day)

**10:30-12:30 PM:** Padel practice (replaces weight training)
**13:00 PM:** Post-padel meal (higher carbs for recovery) + supplements
**17:00 PM:** Standard snack
**21:00 PM:** Standard dinner
**Sleep routine:** Unchanged

### Weekend Schedule (Saturday-Sunday)

**06:30 AM:** Morning run (maintain daily consistency)
**Morning:** Flexible family time
**Optional:** Second padel session or complete rest from structured exercise
**Meal timing:** Maintain 13:00/17:00/21:00 structure when possible
**Sleep routine:** Consistent timing ±30 minutes

## WEEKLY TRAINING SPLIT

### Training Schedule Overview

-   **Monday:** Padel practice (10:30-12:30) + daily run
-   **Tuesday:** Upper Body A (Chest/Triceps) + daily run
-   **Wednesday:** Lower Body (Maintenance/Hormonal) + daily run
-   **Thursday:** Upper Body B (Back/Biceps) + daily run
-   **Friday:** Upper Body A (Chest/Triceps) + daily run
-   **Saturday:** Optional padel/complete rest + daily run
-   **Sunday:** Complete rest from weights + daily run

### Cardiovascular Training Protocol

**Daily Running Parameters:**

-   **Distance:** 4-5km consistently
-   **Target Pace:** 5:30/km (adjust based on energy: 5:45/km tired, 5:15/km energetic)
-   **Heart Rate Zone:** 65-75% max HR (118-136 bpm at age 39)
-   **Duration:** 22-30 minutes
-   **Intensity:** Conversational pace (Zone 2 for fat oxidation)
-   **Non-negotiable:** Provides crucial mood elevation and stress management

**Padel Integration:**

-   **Frequency:** 2-3 sessions per week (Monday practice + thursday night game + ocasioanl saturday morning game)
-   **Intensity:** High-intensity intervals naturally occurring
-   **Benefits:** Anaerobic conditioning, sports performance, social/stress relief
-   **Scheduling:** Monday replaces weight training to prevent overreaching

## INTERMITTENT FASTING PROTOCOL

### Eating Window: 12:30-21:00 (8.5 hours)

**Fasting Period:** 21:00-12:30 (15.5 hours)

### Fasting Window Management (21:00-12:30)

**Allowed:**

-   Water (3-4 liters daily total)
-   Black coffee (optional, for alertness)
-   Herbal teas (chamomile, peppermint, etc.)
-   Electrolyte supplements (sodium, potassium, magnesium)
-   Zero-calorie beverages (sparkling water)

**Hydration Strategy:**

-   **Morning (06:30-12:30):** 1.5-2 liters during fasting period
-   **Eating window (12:30-21:00):** 1.5-2 liters with meals
-   **Pre-workout:** 500ml water 30 minutes before training
-   **Post-workout:** 150% of fluid lost or 750ml within 2 hours

### Breaking the Fast Protocol

**12:30 PM:** Post-workout protein shake marks fast break

-   **Purpose:** Immediate muscle protein synthesis after fasted training
-   **Composition:** 25-30g whey protein + simple carbs + creatine
-   **Timing:** Within 30 minutes of workout completion

## TRAINING TIMING RATIONALE

### Fasted Training Benefits (11:00-12:00 AM)

**Physiological Advantages:**

-   4.5 hours post-run enhances fat oxidation
-   Depleted liver glycogen forces greater fat utilization
-   Improved insulin sensitivity for nutrient partitioning
-   Natural growth hormone elevation during fasted state

**Performance Optimization:**

-   Morning run primes metabolism and mood
-   4.5-hour gap allows energy stabilization
-   Immediate post-workout nutrition optimizes recovery
-   Fits family schedule constraints perfectly

### Training Split Rationale

**Upper Body Focus (2 sessions/week each):**

-   Primary physique goal emphasis
-   Balanced push/pull development
-   Adequate recovery between sessions

**Lower Body Integration (1 session/week):**

-   Hormonal optimization for upper body growth
-   Strength maintenance without excessive size gains
-   Heavy compound movements for testosterone/GH release
-   Low volume, high intensity approach

## SLEEP OPTIMIZATION SCHEDULE

### Target Sleep Metrics

**Duration:** 7-8 hours nightly (23:00-06:30)  
**Quality:** Optimized through environment and supplements  
**Consistency:** ±30 minutes variance including weekends

### Evening Routine (21:30-23:00)

**21:30-22:00:** Preparation Phase

-   Dim lighting by 50%, complete daily tasks
-   5-minute stress journal, electronic wind-down

**22:00-22:30:** Transition Phase

-   Warm shower/bath, light stretching
-   Bedroom environment setup (65-68°F, darkness, white noise)
-   Sleep supplements administration

**22:30-23:00:** Wind-Down Phase

-   Progressive muscle relaxation or breathing exercises
-   Light reading (physical books), gratitude practice
-   Final bathroom visit, complete darkness

### Baby Disruption Management

**Sleep Shift Coordination:** Alternate with partner

-   **Option A:** Parent A (20:00-01:30), Parent B (01:30-07:00)
-   **Option B:** Alternate full nights when possible
-   **Recovery:** Strategic 20-30 minute naps when severely disrupted

## SCHEDULE FLEXIBILITY PROTOCOLS

### Daily Flexibility (±1 hour adjustments)

**Acceptable Variations:**

-   Training time: 10:00-13:00 window
-   First meal: 11:30-13:30 window
-   Dinner time: 20:00-22:00 window
-   Sleep time: 22:30-24:00 window

### Weekly Adaptations

**High Stress Weeks:**

-   Reduce training intensity, maintain frequency
-   Prioritize sleep over early morning workouts if needed
-   Flexible meal timing within eating window

**Travel/Disruption Weeks:**

-   Maintain daily running if possible (hotel gym/outdoor)
-   Bodyweight upper body routine if gym unavailable
-   Keep eating window duration, allow timing shifts

### Emergency Protocols

**Sick Days:**

-   Complete rest from exercise until recovered
-   Maintain hydration and nutrition timing
-   Resume gradually with running first, then weights

**Family Emergencies:**

-   Minimum viable dose: 15-20 minute workouts
-   Flexible meal timing, maintain protein priorities
-   Sleep optimization becomes even more critical

This schedule framework provides structure while maintaining the flexibility essential for sustainable long-term adherence.
