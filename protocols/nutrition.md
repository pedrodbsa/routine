## HIGH-INTENSITY DAY ADJUSTMENTS

**Long run days or hard interval days:** Add 200-300 calories from carbs

**Example additions:**
- Extra 100g rice at lunch (+130 cal, +28g carbs)
- Extra banana post-workout (+100 cal, +25g carbs)
- 50g oats in shake (+190 cal, +32g carbs)

---

## TRACKING & ADJUSTMENTS

**Weekly Check-ins:**
- Weigh daily, track 7-day average
- Target: 0.4-0.6kg loss per week
- Photos every 2 weeks

**If weight loss stalls (2+ weeks):**
1. Verify tracking accuracy
2. Reduce calories by 100 (cut from carbs)
3. Add 10 min daily walking

**If energy crashes:**
1. Add 20-30g carbs around training
2. Check sleep quality
3. Consider diet break (1 week at maintenance)

**If strength drops significantly:**
1. Increase protein to 160g
2. Add carbs pre-workout
3. Accept some strength loss is normal during deficit

---

## RACE WEEK NUTRITION

**Week before:**
- Return to maintenance calories (2,200)
- Increase carbs to 200g+
- Reduce fiber slightly
- Stay well hydrated

**Night before:**
- Familiar foods only
- Moderate carbs, low fiber
- Early dinner (19:00-20:00)

**Race morning:**
- Light breakfast 2-3h before start
- 50-80g easy carbs (banana, toast, oats)
- 500ml water
- Avoid anything new
