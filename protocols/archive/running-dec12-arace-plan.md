# Archived — the Dec 12 2026 10K A-race plan (retired 2026-09-29)

> Moved out of `running.md` and `training.md` by the 2026-09-29 `/consult`. The athlete redefined the objective ("athletic" by summer 2027; body comp > run fitness > racing) and demoted the S. Silvestre Coimbra 10K on Dec 12 to a leisure run with friends. Nothing here is live. It is kept because the evening-race logistics are reusable if the athlete ever runs an evening race hard, and because the peak-block design shows what a 10K-specific block looked like in this stack. Design spec that produced it: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-07-15-december-10k-a-race-design.md`. Consult record: `logbook/2026-09/consult-2026-09-29.md`.

## What it was

- **A-race:** S. Silvestre Coimbra, Sat Dec 12 2026, 18:30 start. **Target sub-47:00 (stretch 46:00)**; 2025 result ~54:00. Adopted 2026-07-31 when the A-race moved off the hilly Oct 25 course.
- **Phase 7 — 10K Peak (Oct 26 – Dec 12):** 45–55 km/wk, two quality sessions per week plus selective VO2max work, taper the last ~10–14 days. Strength: 3 sessions (Push, Pull, Legs) weeks 1–5 (Oct 26 – Nov 29), 2 sessions (Push + Pull, drop Legs) week 6 (Nov 30 – Dec 6), 1 light upper pump session (RPE 5) in race week (Dec 7–12).
- **Nutrition:** maintenance 2,500–2,650 through the peak, race week 2,650–2,750 with protein 160 g.
- **Validation milestones:** Garmin 10K prediction trending sub-50 through Phase 6, sub-48 pace work in Phase 7, confirm-or-re-anchor checkpoint after the Oct 25 B-race (Sep 7 proposal 1).

## Race execution (as written in `running.md`, verbatim)

- Pacing: start 4:50/km, settle 4:42-4:46 by km 3, hold through km 8, kick km 9-10 (stretch pacing: 4:36-4:39 if fitness supports it).
- No in-race fueling needed
- Final 10 days: reduce volume, not intensity. Keep one short 10K-pace touchpoint each week.

**Evening-start logistics (18:30 — this is not a morning race day):**

- **Fueling:** normal carb-forward breakfast and lunch (this is where the race-day carbs live, not a race breakfast); light familiar meal ~15:00-15:30 (~1 g/kg carbs, low fiber/fat); optional 20-30 g easy carbs ~17:30. Nothing new on race day.
- **Caffeine: ~230 mg ~17:45-18:00.** This is a deliberate exception to the ~10-11:00 caffeine cutoff — accept the degraded post-race night as a known one-off cost; do not read the Dec 12-13 sleep data as a signal.
- **Hydration:** sip through the day to clear urine by mid-afternoon (chronic under-hydration flag); ~500 mL with sodium 60-90 min pre-start, then stop — avoid a full bladder at the line.
- **Conditions:** December evening in Coimbra ≈ 8-12 °C, full dark. Warm-up 15-20 min jog + 4-6 strides, finishing as close to the start as possible; keep a warm layer on until the last minutes. Cold start favors a slightly longer warm-up, not a faster opening km.
- **Day structure:** stay off the legs in the afternoon; a normal short morning shakeout (10-15 min + 2 strides) is fine and helps burn off race-day nerves.
- Practice the full evening-race day once in Phase 7 (a Saturday ~18:30 quality session run on the race-day eating schedule).

## Why it was retired

The athlete's statement at the consult: "the race became less important to me. The December race will actually be a leisure race with friends. As such, my goal is to get in a better shape … I want to get to next summer in the best shape possible." The sub-47 target moved to a spring 2027 10K (raced at ~70–71.5 kg after the Jan–Apr cut) and 45:00 became an autumn 2027 stretch. If Dec 12 is run as a solo hard effort (decision due Dec 5), the § Evening-start logistics above apply and the result is recorded as a fitness read, not scored.
