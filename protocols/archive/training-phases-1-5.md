# Training — Phases 1-5 templates (archived 2026-09-29)

Moved out of `protocols/training.md`. Phases 1-5 ran Mar 30 – Sep 20 2026; the live structure is `training.md` § Phases 6-10.

## Phase 1 - Post-HM Recovery (Mar 30 - Apr 12)

Week 1: rest Mon-Wed, then 3 easy runs of 4-5 km; one light full-body session mid-week only if soreness was low. Week 2: 3 sessions with conservative hypertrophy loading (easy 4-7 km runs, light full-body or Push, optional Legs light and Pull, Saturday rest, no quality).

## Phase 2 - Base Rebuild + Cut (Apr 13 - May 4)

Mon easy + Push (PM) · Tue quality · Wed easy + Pull (PM) · Thu easy or rest + Legs · Fri easy · Sat rest · Sun long 12-14 km. Deload: autoregulated mini-deload only if ACWR, soreness or sleep justified it.

## Phase 3 - May Race Block (May 5 - May 29)

Week 1 centred on the May 17 10K, week 2 on the May 24 trail 14.7 km; no heavy legs from Wednesday onward in either race week.

Two quality sessions a week (one harder, one lighter), ≥72 h apart. Upper objective in the deficit: retention first, growth where it comes, small muscles at ~6-9 direct sets/week via the accessory slots (in practice side delts sat at 3). A standing arms/shoulders session was added 2026-07-31 and retired 2026-09-29, never delivered.

Deloads: Phase 4 — one reduced-load week could coincide with the June benchmark race. Phase 5 — synchronized deload every 3rd week (running −15-20%, one quality, lifting −35-40%).

Phase 5 HRV rule: 7-day HRV average >10% below the balanced-low bound → remove that week's second quality session.
