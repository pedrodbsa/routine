## Weekly rules by phase

- Phase 1: easy-only running. Phase 2: one quality session per week, long run on the weekend. Phase 3: sharpen, race, recover; no hard legs in the last 3 days before a race.
- Phases 4-5: two quality sessions per week (one harder, one lighter), ≥72 h apart; long run on the weekend; Legs ≥36 h before the next quality run.
- Phase 4 volume note: the 30-35 km cap was lifted to 35-42 on 2026-06-03 so easy km could sit beside two qualities; long run 12-14 km (≤~35-40% of the week).
- Phase 4 weeks 1-3: harder quality = sub-T / Norwegian; 10K-pace from week 4.
- Q2 survivability rule (2026-06-12): on a compromised week the second quality converted to 6×20 s strides or 6×10-15 s hill sprints on an easy run, counted as delivered.
- Phase 5: volume cap 40 km/week; 7-day HRV >10% below Garmin's balanced-low bound → the lighter quality became easy; deload every 3rd week (−15-20% volume, one quality).

## Phase 4-5 running scoreboard

Easy pace was off the scoreboard until the Sep 21 reversal. The cut scoreboard was: control-run pace-at-HR stable within ~2%; both qualities at target HR; Garmin 10K prediction ≤~50:00-50:30 as the VO2max proxy; volume ≥30 km.
