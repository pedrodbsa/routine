# SUPPLEMENT PROTOCOL

## DAILY SCHEDULE

**12:30 - Post-Workout**
- Creatine Monohydrate: 5g
- Vitamin D3 + K2: 4000-6000 IU D3 / 100-120mcg K2
- Multi-vitamin: 1 tablet

**13:00 - Lunch**
- Folate (L-Methylfolate): 800mcg
- Vitamin B12 (Methylcobalamin): 1000mcg
- Omega-3 Fish Oil: 2g EPA/DHA
- Zinc: 20mg

**21:00 - Dinner**
- Ashwagandha KSM-66: 600mg
- Astaxanthin: 8mg
- Curcumin: 1000mg (with piperine)

**22:30 - Sleep**
- Magnesium Glycinate: 400mg
- L-Theanine: 300mg

## SUPPLEMENT RATIONALE

### Multi-vitamin
**Purpose:** Micronutrient gaps, cognitive health
**Evidence:** COSMOS trial (2024) showed cognitive benefits in older adults

### Folate + B12 (Methylated forms)
**Purpose:** Energy metabolism, red blood cells, nervous system
**Why daily:** Water-soluble, not stored, critical for athletes

### Creatine Monohydrate
**Purpose:** Strength, power, muscle retention during deficit
**Form:** Pure monohydrate (Creapure gold standard)

### Vitamin D3 + K2
**Purpose:** Bone health, immune function, testosterone support
**Synergy:** K2 directs calcium to bones, prevents arterial calcification

### Omega-3 Fish Oil
**Purpose:** Anti-inflammatory, heart health, joint support, recovery
**Form:** Triglyceride form preferred, refrigerate

### Zinc
**Purpose:** Testosterone, immune function, protein synthesis
**Form:** Bisglycinate or picolinate

### Ashwagandha KSM-66
**Purpose:** Cortisol reduction, stress management, recovery, VO2max improvement
**Form:** KSM-66 extract specifically

### Astaxanthin
**Purpose:** Antioxidant, exercise recovery, oxidative stress reduction
**Form:** Natural from Haematococcus pluvialis algae

### Curcumin
**Purpose:** Anti-inflammatory, joint health, recovery
**Form:** With piperine (5-10mg) or phytosome for absorption

### Magnesium Glycinate
**Purpose:** Sleep quality, muscle relaxation, stress reduction
**Form:** Glycinate (gentle on stomach, good for sleep)

### L-Theanine
**Purpose:** Relaxation without sedation, sleep onset
**Note:** Works synergistically with magnesium

## FINASTERIDE COMPATIBILITY

All supplements safe with finasteride:
- No known interactions
- Zinc and ashwagandha support healthy testosterone
- Curcumin has mild 5α-reductase properties (potentially additive for hair)
- Vitamin D supports testosterone without increasing DHT risk
