## AS-NEEDED (Not Daily)

**Curcumin: 1000mg (with piperine)**

- Take only when experiencing joint pain, inflammation, or muscle soreness that persists >48h
- Do NOT take daily during heavy training blocks — may blunt training adaptations by suppressing the inflammatory signaling cascade that drives muscular and cardiovascular adaptation (Trappe et al., 2013; Niklas et al., 2021)
- Safe with finasteride; has mild 5α-reductase properties (potentially additive for hair)

## SUPPLEMENT RATIONALE

### Multi-vitamin

**Purpose:** Micronutrient insurance, cognitive health
**Evidence:** COSMOS trial (2024) showed cognitive benefits. Serves as baseline B-vitamin coverage now that individual B12/folate are paused.
**Note:** Check label to ensure no excessive zinc overlap (stay under 40mg total zinc/day).

### Creatine Monohydrate

**Purpose:** Strength, power, muscle retention during deficit
**Form:** Pure monohydrate (Creapure gold standard)
**Timing:** Post-workout for slightly superior uptake (Candow et al., 2022). On rest days, take with first meal.

### Vitamin D3 + K2

**Purpose:** Bone health, immune function, testosterone support
**Synergy:** K2 directs calcium to bones, prevents arterial calcification
**Note:** High-dose D3 (6000 IU) warranted given indoor WFH lifestyle in Portugal (winter). Reassess dose with bloodwork.

### Omega-3 Fish Oil

**Purpose:** Anti-inflammatory (via resolution pathways, not suppression), heart health, joint support, recovery
**Form:** Triglyceride form preferred, refrigerate
**Note:** Works differently from curcumin/NSAIDs — promotes resolution of inflammation rather than blocking it, so does NOT blunt training adaptations.

### Zinc

**Purpose:** Testosterone, immune function, protein synthesis
**Form:** Bisglycinate or picolinate
**Warning:** Combined with multivitamin, ensure total intake stays under UL of 40mg/day.

### Astaxanthin

**Purpose:** Antioxidant, general health
**Form:** Natural from Haematococcus pluvialis algae
**Note:** Evidence for exercise recovery is limited (small trials). Kept because already purchased and no evidence of harm. Do not repurchase when supply runs out unless new evidence emerges.

### Magnesium

**Purpose:** Muscle relaxation, recovery, sleep support
**Form issue:** Glycinate was causing difficulty falling asleep (paradoxical reaction — possibly due to glycine acting as NMDA co-agonist in some individuals).
**Options to try:**

- **Magnesium taurate** — best alternative for sleep + cardiovascular benefits
- **Magnesium citrate** — well-absorbed, may have mild laxative effect
- **Move timing earlier** — try taking with dinner (21:00) instead of at bedtime
  **Dose:** 400mg elemental magnesium regardless of form.

## FINASTERIDE COMPATIBILITY

All current supplements safe with finasteride:

- No known interactions
- Zinc supports healthy testosterone
- Curcumin has mild 5α-reductase properties (potentially additive for hair)
- Vitamin D supports testosterone without increasing DHT risk
- Omega-3 is neutral regarding 5AR activity
