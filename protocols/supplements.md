# SUPPLEMENT PROTOCOL - MASTER

> Conservative core stack. Everything listed is compatible with finasteride 1 mg/day. Isotretinoin (microdose) is taken with dinner, the fattiest meal.

## Daily Schedule

### Morning coffee (every day, including rest and travel days)

- **Creatine monohydrate 5 g.** Timing is irrelevant; daily continuity is everything, so it rides the one anchor that exists on all seven days. Never attach it to a shake or any training-contingent feed: that coupling once cost 23 straight days. Restarting after a gap puts 0.5–1.5 kg of intracellular water on the scale over 2–4 weeks; that is not fat, and it lifts the scale's BIA muscle channel too.

### Breakfast (07:00-09:00)

- Vitamin D3 + K2: **2,000-4,000 IU D3** / 100-120 mcg K2 — at or under the 4,000 IU adult upper limit until a 25(OH)D test says otherwise (`calendar.md`).
- Multivitamin: 1 tablet.

### Lunch (~13:00)

- Omega-3 fish oil: 2 g combined EPA/DHA
- Finasteride: 1 mg
- Zinc: 20 mg — **on hold pending a stated reason.** None is on file, and 20 mg plus the multivitamin's zinc plus a meat-heavy diet approaches the 40 mg adult upper limit (chronic excess impairs copper absorption). Check the multivitamin label before resuming.

### Pre-Bed (~22:00)

- Magnesium glycinate: 200-350 mg elemental. The 350 mg supplemental upper limit includes the multivitamin's magnesium. Well tolerated and corrects a common dietary shortfall; its sleep-onset benefit is modest, so it is not a primary sleep lever.

## Strategic / Optional

- **Caffeine:** 200-250 mg before quality sessions or races. **Cutoff ~10:00-11:00 on normal days**, because sleep-onset latency is the primary recovery constraint and the ~5-6 h half-life leaves a meaningful fraction at a 22:00-23:00 bedtime. An afternoon quality session gets its pre-session dose instead of a morning cup.
- **Collagen peptides:** 15 g + ~50 mg vitamin C, 45-60 min before running, in high-volume blocks or if tendon stiffness appears.
- **Astaxanthin:** not a daily supplement during aerobic-development blocks.

## As-Needed Only

- Curcumin 1,000 mg with piperine, only for pain/inflammation lasting >48 h. Not daily during training blocks: chronic anti-inflammatory use can blunt adaptation.
