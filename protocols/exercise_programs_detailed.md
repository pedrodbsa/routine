# COMPLETE EXERCISE PROGRAMS & PROGRESSIONS

## UPPER BODY A: CHEST & TRICEPS FOCUS (Tuesday/Friday)

### Primary Workout Structure (45-60 minutes)
**Warm-up (5-10 minutes):**
- Arm circles: 10 forward, 10 backward
- Band pull-aparts: 2 sets × 15 reps
- Push-ups: 1 set × 8-12 reps (activation)
- Light rowing motion: 1 set × 10-15 reps

### Main Exercises

**1. Barbell/Dumbbell Bench Press**
- **Sets:** 4 sets × 6-8 reps
- **Rest:** 2-3 minutes between sets
- **Progression:** Increase weight when achieving 8 reps with perfect form
- **Form Cues:** 2-second descent, 1-second pause, explosive up
- **Alternative:** Dumbbell version if shoulder discomfort

**2. Incline Dumbbell Press (30-45°)**  
- **Sets:** 4 sets × 8-10 reps
- **Rest:** 2-3 minutes between sets
- **Focus:** Upper chest development and definition
- **Form Cues:** Natural arm path, squeeze at top
- **Weight Selection:** 70-80% of flat bench weight

**3. Weighted Dips**
- **Sets:** 3 sets × 10-12 reps
- **Progression:** Bodyweight → belt weight → higher reps
- **Rest:** 90 seconds between sets
- **Form Cues:** Lean forward slightly, full range of motion
- **Alternative:** Assisted dips if needed initially

**4. Close-Grip Bench Press**
- **Sets:** 3 sets × 8-10 reps
- **Rest:** 2 minutes between sets
- **Grip:** Hands shoulder-width apart, elbows tucked
- **Focus:** Triceps mass development
- **Weight:** 80-85% of regular bench press

**5. Cable Crossovers**
- **Sets:** 3 sets × 12-15 reps
- **Rest:** 60-90 seconds between sets
- **Focus:** Chest definition and separation
- **Form:** Constant tension, peak contraction emphasis
- **Variation:** High to low angle for different chest areas

**6. Overhead Triceps Extension**
- **Sets:** 3 sets × 10-12 reps
- **Rest:** 60-90 seconds between sets
- **Focus:** Long head triceps development
- **Equipment:** Dumbbell or cable
- **Form:** Full range of motion, control the negative

### Cool-down (5 minutes)
- Chest doorway stretch: 30 seconds each arm
- Triceps overhead stretch: 30 seconds each arm
- Cross-body shoulder stretch: 30 seconds each arm

## UPPER BODY B: BACK & BICEPS FOCUS (Thursday)

### Primary Workout Structure (45-60 minutes)
**Warm-up (5-10 minutes):**
- Arm swings: 10 each direction
- Band rows: 2 sets × 15 reps
- Scapular wall slides: 1 set × 10 reps
- Light lat pulldown: 1 set × 10-15 reps

### Main Exercises

**1. Pull-ups/Lat Pulldowns**
- **Sets:** 4 sets × 6-10 reps
- **Progression:** Assisted → bodyweight → weighted
- **Rest:** 2-3 minutes between sets
- **Focus:** Lat width and V-taper development
- **Form:** Full range, squeeze shoulder blades

**2. Barbell/Dumbbell Rows**
- **Sets:** 4 sets × 8-10 reps
- **Rest:** 2-3 minutes between sets
- **Focus:** Back thickness, rhomboids, mid-traps
- **Form:** Chest up, squeeze shoulder blades together
- **Variation:** Chest-supported to eliminate momentum

**3. T-Bar Rows**
- **Sets:** 3 sets × 10-12 reps
- **Rest:** 90 seconds between sets
- **Focus:** Mid-trap definition and back detail
- **Setup:** Use barbell in corner or T-bar apparatus
- **Form:** Unique angle for complete back development

**4. Barbell/Dumbbell Curls**
- **Sets:** 4 sets × 8-10 reps
- **Rest:** 90 seconds between sets
- **Focus:** Biceps mass development
- **Form:** Controlled tempo, full range, avoid momentum
- **Progression:** Increase weight when form remains perfect

**5. Hammer Curls**
- **Sets:** 3 sets × 10-12 reps
- **Rest:** 60-90 seconds between sets
- **Focus:** Brachialis and biceps peak development
- **Grip:** Neutral grip throughout movement
- **Form:** Peak contraction emphasis

**6. Face Pulls**
- **Sets:** 3 sets × 12-15 reps
- **Rest:** 60 seconds between sets
- **Focus:** Posterior delts, external rotators
- **Cable Height:** Upper chest level
- **Form:** Pull to face, squeeze rear delts

### Cool-down (5 minutes)
- Lat stretch against wall: 30 seconds each arm
- Biceps doorway stretch: 30 seconds each arm
- Upper trap stretch: 30 seconds each side

## LOWER BODY: MAINTENANCE & HORMONAL OPTIMIZATION (Wednesday)

### Primary Workout Structure (40-50 minutes)
**Purpose:** Maximize hormonal benefits while maintaining leg strength without excessive size gains

**Warm-up (5-10 minutes):**
- Leg swings: 10 forward/back, 10 side-to-side each leg
- Bodyweight squats: 2 sets × 10 reps
- Walking lunges: 10 each leg
- Hip circles: 10 each direction

### Main Exercises (Strength Focus - Low Volume, High Intensity)

**1. Barbell Back Squats**
- **Sets:** 4 sets × 3-5 reps
- **Rest:** 3-4 minutes between sets
- **Focus:** Maximum hormonal response, strength maintenance
- **Form:** Full depth, explosive up, controlled descent
- **Weight:** 85-90% of 1RM for hormonal benefits

**2. Romanian Deadlifts**
- **Sets:** 3 sets × 5-6 reps
- **Rest:** 2-3 minutes between sets
- **Focus:** Hamstring/glute strength, hormone optimization
- **Form:** Hip hinge pattern, feel stretch in hamstrings
- **Weight:** Heavy but controlled

**3. Bulgarian Split Squats**
- **Sets:** 2 sets × 8-10 reps each leg
- **Rest:** 90 seconds between sets
- **Focus:** Unilateral strength, stability
- **Form:** Controlled tempo, full range of motion
- **Weight:** Bodyweight or light dumbbells

**4. Walking Lunges**
- **Sets:** 2 sets × 12 steps each leg
- **Rest:** 60 seconds between sets
- **Focus:** Functional movement, light metabolic stress
- **Form:** Step into lunge, drive through heel
- **Weight:** Bodyweight or light dumbbells

**5. Calf Raises**
- **Sets:** 3 sets × 15-20 reps
- **Rest:** 60 seconds between sets
- **Focus:** Lower leg strength and definition
- **Form:** Full range of motion, pause at top
- **Weight:** Bodyweight or light added weight

### Cool-down (5 minutes)
- Quad stretch: 30 seconds each leg
- Hamstring stretch: 30 seconds each leg
- Calf stretch: 30 seconds each leg
- Hip flexor stretch: 30 seconds each leg

**Key Protocol Notes:**
- **Low Volume:** Prevents excessive leg growth
- **High Intensity:** Maximizes hormonal response
- **Compound Focus:** Squats and deadlifts for maximum testosterone/GH release
- **Short Rest:** Maintains efficiency while allowing proper recovery

## PROGRESSION PROTOCOLS

### Progressive Overload Strategy

**Weeks 1-2: Form Mastery**
- Focus on perfect technique
- Establish baseline weights
- RPE 6-7 (moderate effort)
- Full range of motion emphasis

**Weeks 3-4: Linear Progression**
- Increase weight when hitting upper rep range
- RPE 7-8 (challenging but controlled)
- Add 2.5-5kg for compound movements
- Add 1-2.5kg for isolation movements

**Weeks 5-6: Intensity Techniques**
- Rest-pause sets on final set of each exercise
- Tempo manipulation (3-second negatives)
- RPE 8-9 (near maximum effort)

**Weeks 7-8: Deload Week**
- Reduce volume by 40-50%
- Maintain movement patterns
- Focus on mobility and recovery
- Prepare for next progression cycle

### Alternative Progression Methods
**When Weight Progression Stalls:**
1. **Volume Progression:** Add one additional set
2. **Density Progression:** Reduce rest periods by 15-30 seconds
3. **Range Progression:** Increase range of motion
4. **Tempo Progression:** Slower negatives or paused reps

### Exercise Substitutions

**For Shoulder Issues:**
- Bench Press → Dumbbell Press or Machine Press
- Overhead Extensions → Rope Pushdowns
- Dips → Machine Dips or Push-ups

**For Equipment Limitations:**
- Barbell Rows → Dumbbell Rows or Cable Rows
- T-Bar Rows → Single-arm Dumbbell Rows
- Barbell Curls → Dumbbell Curls or Cable Curls

## CARDIOVASCULAR TRAINING DETAILS

### Daily Running Protocol
**Pre-Run Preparation (5 minutes):**
- Dynamic leg swings: 10 each direction
- High knees march: 20 steps
- Butt kicks: 20 steps
- Light jogging: 2-3 minutes

**Main Run Parameters:**
- **Distance:** 4-5km (consistent daily)
- **Target Pace:** 5:30/km (adjust ±15 seconds based on energy)
- **Heart Rate:** 65-75% max HR (118-136 bpm)
- **Breathing:** Should be able to speak in short sentences
- **Surface:** Vary between road, trail, treadmill for joint health

**Post-Run Recovery (5 minutes):**
- Walk cooldown: 2-3 minutes
- Calf stretches: 30 seconds each leg
- Quad stretches: 30 seconds each leg
- Hip flexor stretches: 30 seconds each leg

### Running Progression Options
**If Current Pace Becomes Too Easy:**
- Maintain 5:30/km for fat oxidation benefits
- Add hills or intervals once weekly (Saturday)
- Extend distance to 6km on weekends only
- Focus on consistency over intensity

## INJURY PREVENTION & MODIFICATION

### Daily Mobility Routine (10 minutes)
**Morning (Pre-Run):**
- Cat-cow stretches: 10 reps
- Hip circles: 5 each direction
- Arm swings: 10 each direction
- Leg swings: 10 each direction

**Post-Workout:**
- Doorway chest stretch: 1 minute
- Overhead lat stretch: 30 seconds each arm
- Hip flexor stretch: 30 seconds each leg
- Thoracic spine rotation: 10 each direction

### Load Management Guidelines
**Weekly Volume Targets:**
- Total sets per muscle group: 12-16 sets weekly
- Training days per week: 6 (4 weights + 7 cardio)
- Rest days: 1 complete rest, others active recovery

**Fatigue Management:**
- RPE should not exceed 9 on any set
- If performance drops >10%, end session
- Prioritize sleep over additional training
- Listen to body signals for overreaching

### Recovery Optimization
**Between Sets:**
- Deep breathing exercises
- Light stretching of non-working muscles
- Hydration (small sips)
- Mental preparation for next set

**Between Workouts:**
- Contrast showers (hot/cold alternating)
- Light walking on rest days
- Foam rolling or self-massage
- Adequate protein intake within 2 hours

This exercise program provides the framework for achieving your lean, defined physique while accommodating real-world constraints and maintaining long-term sustainability.
