# COMPLETE SUPPLEMENT SCHEDULE & PROTOCOLS

## DAILY SUPPLEMENT TIMING SCHEDULE

### 12:30 PM (Post-Workout)

**Creatine Monohydrate: 5g**

-   **Form:** Pure creatine monohydrate powder
-   **Timing Rationale:** Post-workout with carbs enhances uptake
-   **Mixing:** Add to protein shake for convenience
-   **Expected Benefits:** Increased strength, power, muscle volume

**Vitamin D3: 6000 IU + Vitamin K2 (MK-7): 120mcg**

-   **Form:** 2 x 3000 IU capsules (using existing supply) + K2 supplement
-   **Timing Rationale:** Fat-soluble, best with post-workout meal
-   **Synergy:** K2 ensures proper calcium utilization
-   **Hair Safety:** Higher dose for testosterone optimization; finasteride provides DHT protection
-   **Expected Benefits:** Enhanced testosterone support, bone health, immune function

### 13:00 PM (With Lunch)

**B-Complex (Methylated Forms - High Potency)**

-   **Key Components:** B1 (25mg), B6 (P5P form, 25mg), B12 (Methylcobalamin, 1000mcg), Folate (L-5-MTHF, 800mcg)
-   **Timing Rationale:** Supports energy metabolism, taken with largest meal
-   **Form:** Methylated forms for better absorption and efficacy
-   **Expected Benefits:** Enhanced energy production, nervous system support, better absorption

**Omega-3 Fish Oil: 2g EPA/DHA**

-   **Ratio:** 2:1 or 3:2 EPA to DHA
-   **Form:** Triglyceride form preferred over ethyl ester
-   **Storage:** Refrigerated to prevent oxidation
-   **Expected Benefits:** Anti-inflammatory, hormone optimization, recovery

**Zinc Bisglycinate: 20mg**

-   **Form:** Chelated zinc for superior absorption
-   **Timing:** With food to prevent stomach upset
-   **Separation:** Taken with lunch, away from other minerals
-   **Hair Safety:** Increased dose for testosterone support; reasonable level when on finasteride
-   **Expected Benefits:** Testosterone support, immune function, protein synthesis

**Finasteride: 1mg**

-   **Timing:** Consistent daily timing with lunch
-   **Interaction Check:** No known interactions with other supplements
-   **Consistency:** Same time daily for optimal effectiveness
-   **Purpose:** Hair loss prevention, DHT regulation

### 21:00 PM (With Dinner)

**Ashwagandha KSM-66: 600mg**

-   **Form:** KSM-66 proprietary extract (most researched)
-   **Timing Rationale:** Evening for cortisol reduction, stress management
-   **Quality:** Look for "KSM-66" on label for clinical dosing
-   **Expected Benefits:** Stress reduction, testosterone support, sleep quality

**Astaxanthin: 8mg**

-   **Form:** Natural astaxanthin from Haematococcus pluvialis
-   **Timing:** With dietary fat for absorption
-   **Quality:** Natural vs synthetic (natural preferred)
-   **Expected Benefits:** Antioxidant protection, exercise recovery, eye health

**Curcumin with Piperine: 1000mg**

-   **Form:** Standardized 95% curcuminoids with 5mg piperine
-   **Timing:** With dinner fats for optimal absorption
-   **Hair Safety:** Has mild 5α-reductase inhibiting properties (potentially protective)
-   **Expected Benefits:** Anti-inflammatory, recovery enhancement, shoulder pain relief

### 22:30 PM (90 minutes before sleep)

**Magnesium Glycinate: 400mg**

-   **Form:** Chelated magnesium for minimal GI upset
-   **Timing:** 90 minutes before sleep for optimal absorption
-   **Alternative Forms:** Magnesium citrate or threonate if glycinate unavailable
-   **Expected Benefits:** Sleep quality, muscle relaxation, stress reduction

**L-Theanine: 300mg**

-   **Form:** Pure L-theanine powder or capsules
-   **Timing:** Synergistic with magnesium for sleep
-   **Source:** Derived from green tea but without caffeine
-   **Expected Benefits:** Relaxation without sedation, sleep onset

## INTERACTION MANAGEMENT

### Positive Synergies

**Sleep Stack (22:30 PM):**

-   Magnesium + L-Theanine work synergistically
-   Natural sleep promotion without dependency
-   Supports recovery and hormone optimization
-   Note: Glycine can be added later if sleep depth needs improvement

**Stress Management (21:00):**

-   Ashwagandha provides comprehensive evening stress reduction
-   Supports cortisol optimization and testosterone enhancement
-   Works synergistically with daily running for stress management

**Hormone Support:**

-   Vitamin D3 (6000 IU) + Zinc (20mg) + Ashwagandha
-   Natural testosterone optimization safe for finasteride users
-   Curcumin provides additional anti-inflammatory support
-   Particularly important for 39-year-old males

### Separation Requirements

**Minerals:**

-   Zinc taken with lunch, magnesium before bed
-   Prevents competition for absorption
-   Iron supplements (if needed) taken separately

**Timing Conflicts:**

-   B-vitamins in morning/lunch (energy support)
-   Sleep supplements in evening only
-   Ashwagandha evening timing prevents sleep interference

### Medication Considerations

**Finasteride Compatibility:**

-   No known interactions with any supplements in protocol
-   Ashwagandha may support testosterone optimization while finasteride blocks DHT
-   Zinc supports healthy hormone metabolism
-   Curcumin has mild 5α-reductase inhibiting properties (potentially hair-protective)
-   Higher vitamin D3 dose is safe due to finasteride's DHT protection
-   Monitor for any unusual effects during first month

**Blood Pressure Medications:**

-   Magnesium may enhance blood pressure lowering effects
-   Monitor if taking BP medications
-   Inform healthcare provider of supplement use

## MONITORING & ADJUSTMENTS

### Weekly Assessment Metrics

**Sleep Quality (1-10 scale):**

-   Target: 7+ consistently after 2 weeks
-   Track sleep onset time and wake quality
-   Note any changes in dream recall or night wakings

**Energy Levels (1-10 scale):**

-   Morning energy upon waking
-   Afternoon energy (post-lunch dip)
-   Pre-workout energy levels
-   Target: 7+ across all time periods

**Stress Management (1-10 scale):**

-   Perceived stress levels
-   Ability to cope with daily stressors
-   Physical stress symptoms (tension, etc.)
-   Target: 6+ improvement from baseline

**Training Performance:**

-   Strength progression consistency
-   Recovery between sessions
-   Workout completion rates
-   Target: No decline from baseline, gradual improvement

### Monthly Optimization Reviews

**Effectiveness Assessment:**

-   Which supplements provide noticeable benefits?
-   Any side effects or tolerance issues?
-   Cost-benefit analysis of each supplement
-   Adjust quantities or timing as needed

**Blood Work Considerations (every 6 months):**

-   Vitamin D levels (target: 40-60 ng/ml)
-   Comprehensive metabolic panel
-   Lipid profile (omega-3 effects)
-   Testosterone levels (finasteride + support stack effects)

### Adjustment Protocols

**If Sleep Doesn't Improve:**

-   Increase magnesium to 600mg
-   Add glycine to 5g
-   Assess bedroom environment factors
-   Consider melatonin alternative (despite hormone concerns)

**If Stress Levels Remain High:**

-   Increase ashwagandha to 900mg temporarily
-   Add meditation or breathing practices
-   Assess lifestyle stressor reduction
-   Consider rhodiola addition (400mg at 17:00) if needed

**If Energy Doesn't Improve:**

-   Check B-vitamin absorption (genetic testing available)
-   Assess iron levels (common deficiency)
-   Verify adequate caloric intake
-   Consider rhodiola addition if afternoon energy crashes persist

**If Training Performance Declines:**

-   Verify creatine saturation (may take 4 weeks)
-   Assess overall recovery factors
-   Check protein and carbohydrate intake
-   Consider HMB addition for muscle preservation
