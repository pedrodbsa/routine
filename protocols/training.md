# TRAINING PROTOCOLS

## WEEKLY STRUCTURE

3 strength sessions/week:
- **1 PT session:** Typically leg-focused, PT-selected exercises
- **2 self-directed:** User chooses exercises and gym location
- **Running:** Garmin Coach half-marathon plan (race March 29, 2025)
- **Schedule flexibility:** Training times vary daily based on availability

## RUNNING (GARMIN PLAN)

Garmin handles all running periodization. Trust the plan.

**Race Target: Sub-2h Half Marathon**
- Race pace: 5:40/km
- Easy runs: 6:00-6:30/km
- Tempo runs: 5:20-5:30/km
- Intervals: 4:50-5:10/km

**Key Principles:**
- Follow suggested workouts (easy, tempo, intervals, long runs)
- Respect recovery recommendations
- Don't add extra volume beyond the plan
- Long runs build toward 18-20km pre-race

## HYROX TRAINING

**Main Gym:** Weights, treadmill, air rower, air bike
**Home Gym:** Free weights, space for lunges, battle ropes, potential sled pulls

### Session Structure (60-75 minutes)

**Warm-up (10 min):**
- 5 min air bike or rowing
- Dynamic stretches
- Movement prep

---

### Option A: Main Gym Session (Cardio Focus)

**Block 1 - Rowing Power (15 min)**
- 4 x 500m air rower @ high effort
- Rest 90 sec between intervals

**Block 2 - Air Bike Intervals (12 min)**
- 8 x 30 sec max effort / 60 sec easy

**Block 3 - Functional Circuit (20 min)**
3 rounds:
- 20 goblet squats (heavy)
- 50m dumbbell farmers carry
- 20 walking lunges
- 15 burpees
- Rest 90 sec

**Block 4 - Treadmill Finisher (10 min)**
- 1km @ race pace (5:40/km) after circuit

---

### Option B: Home Gym Session (Strength/Skills Focus)

**Block 1 - Battle Ropes (10 min)**
- 6 x 30 sec waves / 30 sec rest

**Block 2 - Sled Work (15 min)**
- 6 x 25m sled push (heavy)
- 6 x 25m sled pull
- Rest 60 sec between

**Block 3 - Lunge Endurance (10 min)**
- 4 x 50m walking lunges
- Rest 60 sec between

**Block 4 - Farmers Carry (10 min)**
- 4 x 100m @ 24kg per hand
- Rest 90 sec between

**Block 5 - Thrusters (10 min)**
- 4 x 20 reps @ 15-20kg

## PROGRESSION GUIDELINES

### Race Prep Phase (Now - March 29)

**Priority:** Maintain, don't build
- Keep weights stable, don't push for PRs
- RPE 7-8 maximum (save energy for running)
- If fatigued from running, reduce volume (drop 1 set per exercise)
- Never sacrifice running quality for lifting

### Post-Race (April onwards)

- Increase Hyrox training to 2x/week
- Add lower body strength day
- Progressive overload on all lifts

---

## RECOVERY

**Between Sessions:**
- Sleep: 7-8 hours minimum target
- Hydration: 3-4L daily
- Protein timing: Within 2h of training

**Warning Signs to Back Off:**
- Resting HR elevated >5bpm
- Garmin body battery consistently low
- Persistent muscle soreness >48h
- Sleep quality declining
