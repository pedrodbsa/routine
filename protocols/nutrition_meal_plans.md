## MEAL PREPARATION STRATEGIES

### Weekly Batch Cooking (Sunday & Wednesday, 2 hours each)

**Proteins (Cook in Large Batches):**

-   Grill 8-10 chicken breasts (season with herbs/spices)
-   Bake 6-8 salmon fillets (lemon, garlic, herbs)
-   Cook 2 lbs lean ground beef with basic seasonings
-   Hard boil 12 eggs for quick protein additions

**Carbohydrates (Prepare in Advance):**

-   Cook 4 cups quinoa (stores well, versatile)
-   Bake 4-6 sweet potatoes (reheat easily)
-   Cook 3 cups brown rice (portion into containers)
-   Wash and cut vegetables for quick steaming

**Healthy Fats (Portion Control):**

-   Pre-portion nuts into 20g servings
-   Prepare olive oil-based dressings
-   Slice avocados fresh (don't store)

### Daily Preparation (15 minutes maximum)

**Morning Prep (During kid care):**

-   Assemble post-workout shake ingredients
-   Set out lunch components for quick assembly
-   Prepare snack containers

**Post-Workout (5 minutes):**

-   Blend protein shake immediately
-   Begin lunch preparation

**Afternoon Prep:**

-   Portion snack while preparing family dinner
-   Set up dinner cooking process

### Storage & Food Safety

**Protein Storage:**

-   Cooked proteins: 3-4 days refrigerated in glass containers
-   Raw proteins: Use within 2 days or freeze immediately
-   Eggs: Hard-boiled last 1 week in shell, 3 days peeled

**Carbohydrate Storage:**

-   Cooked grains: 4-5 days refrigerated, freeze up to 3 months
-   Sweet potatoes: 4-5 days refrigerated, reheat in oven
-   Fresh fruits: Buy 2-3 days worth at a time

**Vegetable Storage:**

-   Pre-cut vegetables: 2-3 days maximum in refrigerator
-   Leafy greens: Wash, dry, store in paper towels
-   Frozen vegetables: Keep as backup for busy days

## FOOD QUALITY GUIDELINES

### Protein Source Hierarchy

**Tier 1 (Daily Use):**

-   Chicken breast, wild-caught fish, eggs, Greek yogurt
-   Whey protein powder (post-workout only)
-   Lean cuts of beef and pork

**Tier 2 (Regular Use):**

-   Cottage cheese, lean turkey, canned fish (in water)
-   Plant proteins (if combining for complete amino acids)

**Tier 3 (Occasional):**

-   Processed meats, protein bars
-   Higher fat cuts of meat (account for fat content)

### Carbohydrate Source Hierarchy

**Tier 1 (Primary Sources):**

-   White rice, potatoes, quinoa, oats
-   Fresh fruits (especially post-workout)
-   Vegetables (unlimited leafy greens)

**Tier 2 (Moderate Use):**

-   Sweet potatoes, whole grain bread and pasta
-   Brown rice
-   Legumes and beans

**Tier 3 (Limited Use):**

-   Processed grains and sugary foods
-   Use strategically around training if needed

### Fat Source Hierarchy

**Tier 1 (Emphasis):**

-   Olive oil, avocado, nuts and seeds
-   Fatty fish (salmon, mackerel, sardines)
-   Coconut oil for cooking

**Tier 2 (Moderate):**

-   Grass-fed butter, egg yolks
-   Olives, nut butters

**Tier 3 (Minimize):**

-   Processed oils, fried foods
-   Trans fats (avoid completely)

## FLEXIBLE EATING STRATEGIES

### Social Situations

**Restaurant Guidelines:**

-   Focus on protein and vegetables
-   Ask for sauces and dressings on the side
-   Substitute starches when possible
-   Don't stress about perfect macros for one meal

**Travel Strategies:**

-   Pack protein powder and portable snacks
-   Research restaurant menus in advance
-   Maintain eating window timing when possible
-   Prioritize protein intake above all else

**Special Occasions:**

-   Eat normally beforehand, don't "save calories"
-   Focus on socializing rather than food
-   Make the best choices available
-   Return to normal eating the next meal

### Weekend Flexibility

**Saturday/Sunday Adjustments:**

-   Allow ±2 hour eating window shifts for family activities
-   Maintain overall calorie and protein goals
-   Flexible meal timing for family outings
-   Prepare backup portable meals

## PROGRESS ADJUSTMENT PROTOCOLS

### If Fat Loss Stalls (4+ weeks)

**Step 1:** Reduce total calories by 100-150

-   Remove 20-25g carbohydrates (80-100 calories)
-   Reduce fat by 5-10g (45-90 calories)
-   Maintain protein intake

**Step 2:** Add metabolic flexibility

-   One day per week: reduce carbs to 100g, increase fat to 90g
-   Maintain other days as planned

**Step 3:** Implement refeed strategy

-   Every 2 weeks: one day at maintenance calories (2,500)
-   Extra calories from carbohydrates primarily

### If Energy Drops Significantly

**Assessment Questions:**

-   Sleep quality adequate (7+ hours)?
-   Stress levels manageable?
-   Training intensity too high?
-   Undereating consistently?

**Adjustments:**

-   Increase calories by 100-150 for 1-2 weeks
-   Redistribute carbohydrates around training
-   Consider diet break (1-2 weeks at maintenance)

### If Strength Decreases

**Immediate Actions:**

-   Increase pre/post workout carbohydrates by 15-20g
-   Ensure adequate protein (may increase to 200g)
-   Assess recovery factors (sleep, stress)
-   Consider reducing cardio intensity temporarily

This comprehensive nutrition plan provides the flexibility and structure needed for sustainable body recomposition while maintaining family and social connections.
