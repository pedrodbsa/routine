## WORKOUT TYPES

### Easy/Base Runs

- **HR:** 128-142 (treadmill) | **Pace:** 6:15-7:15/km | **Duration:** 30-60 min | **RPE:** 3-4

### Long Runs

- **HR:** 128-145 (drift max 147) | **Duration:** 60-120 min | **RPE:** 4-5

### Sub-Threshold Intervals (Norwegian Singles)

Sub-threshold (2.3-3.0 mmol/L) intervals yield more adaptation than continuous tempo at threshold (4.0 mmol/L) with less fatigue.

- **Long:** 3×10 min (145-152 bpm, 60-90s rec)
- **Short:** 8-10×3 min (148-155 bpm, 45-60s rec)
- **Micro:** 12-15×90 sec (150-158 bpm, 30-45s rec)
- **Execution:** Stay below gasping — target "controlled discomfort". Include 15 min easy WU/CD.

### 10K-Pace Intervals

- **HR:** 160-172 | **Pace:** 4:36-4:50/km (based on sub-46 target)
- **Structure:** 6-8×1km or 4-5×1.5km at goal 10K pace, 60s jog recovery
- **RPE:** 8-9
- Introduce from Cut Block 1 (May 26). Core 10K-specific session.

### Traditional Tempo (Alternative)

- **HR:** 155-165 | **Pace:** 5:20-5:40/km | **Duration:** 20-40 min continuous | **RPE:** 7-8

### VO2max Intervals

- **HR:** 165-180 work, <130 recovery | **Pace:** 4:50-5:10/km
- **Structure:** 400m-1600m repeats, equal or shorter recovery | **RPE:** 9-10
- 1x/week max during race-specific phase

### X-Element (Neuromuscular)

- **Hill Sprints:** 6-8×10-15s max effort uphill, full recovery
- **Strides:** 6-8×20s accelerations at end of easy runs
- **Short Repeats:** 6-8×200m at mile pace, full recovery

## MONITORING

### RPE Scale

| RPE  | Level     | Talk Test           | Use For                        |
| ---- | --------- | ------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| 3-4  | Easy      | Full conversation   | Easy/base runs, recovery       |
| 5-6  | Moderate  | Sentences w/ pauses | **AVOID on easy days**         |
| 7-8  | Hard      | Short phrases       | Tempo, sub-threshold, strength |
| 9-10 | Very hard | Words / silence     | VO2max, race kick              |

### ACWR (Acute:Chronic Workload Ratio)

Acute (this week's km) ÷ Chronic (4-week rolling avg). Compute weekly.
| ACWR | Status | Action |
| ------ | ---------- | ------------------------------- |
| 0.8-1.3 | Sweet spot | Optimal |
| 1.3-1.5 | Caution | Monitor fatigue |
| >1.5 | Danger | Reduce volume |
| <0.8 | Detraining | Increase gradually |

### Warning Signs (Back Off)

- RHR elevated >5 bpm for 3+ days
- Easy runs need >150 bpm for normal pace
- Garmin Sleep score consistently <60
- Body battery not recovering above 50
- Persistent leg heaviness or unusual fatigue

## RACE DAY

### 10K (October 25 A-Race)

- Warm-up: 15-20 min jog + 4-6 strides
- Pacing: Start at 4:45/km (conservative), settle to 4:36-4:39 by km 3, hold through km 8, kick km 9-10
- No in-race fueling needed (<50 min)
- Caffeine: ~230mg 30-60 min pre-race
- Target: Sub-46:00 (realistic) | Sub-45:00 (stretch)

### Half Marathon (Historical Reference)

- Practice gels during long runs >90 min
- Carb load 2-3 days before, hydration morning of
- Warm-up: 10-15 min jog + 2-4 strides
- Pacing: Even effort, save energy for final 5K

### Recovery Between Hard Sessions

- 2-3 days between quality workouts (incl. long runs)
- Fatigue >48h → extend recovery or reduce next session
