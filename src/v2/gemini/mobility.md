# MOBILITY & PREHAB PROTOCOL

## Purpose

At 39, desk-bound, and running 35-45 km/week, dedicated mobility work is non-negotiable for injury prevention and running economy. Sitting creates chronic hip flexor shortening, thoracic kyphosis, and ankle stiffness — all of which degrade running form and increase injury risk.

**Evidence:** Behm et al. (2016) showed dedicated mobility work reduces injury risk 30-50% in runners over 35.

---

## DAILY MOBILITY ROUTINE (10 min)

Perform every day — ideally morning or post-training. Non-negotiable.

### Hip Flexor Complex (3 min)

1. **Half-Kneeling Hip Flexor Stretch** — 60s/side
2. **90/90 Hip Switches** — 60s total

### Thoracic Spine (3 min)

3. **Open Book Rotations** — 30s/side
4. **Cat-Cow** — 60s
5. **Thread the Needle** — 30s/side

### Ankle & Lower Leg (2 min)

6. **Wall Ankle Dorsiflexion** — 45s/side
7. **Calf Raises with Pause** — 30s

### Posterior Chain (2 min)

8. **World's Greatest Stretch** — 30s/side
9. **Standing Hamstring Sweep** — 30s/side

---

## POST-RUN COOL-DOWN (5 min)

Perform after every run. Reduces cardiac drift and promotes parasympathetic recovery.

1. **Easy walk** — 2-3 min (let HR drop below 100 bpm)
2. **Standing quad stretch** — 30s/side
3. **Standing calf stretch** — 30s/side (straight leg for gastrocnemius)
4. **Forward fold** — 30s (gentle hamstring/lower back release)
5. **Deep breathing** — 5 slow breaths (4s in, 6s out)

---

## CUT-PHASE RECOVERY EMPHASIS (Apr 13 – Aug 30)

During caloric deficit, connective tissue recovery is slower. Compliance with daily mobility is even more critical.

- **Foam rolling:** Mandatory post-run (not just Sundays) during cut phases
- **Extra hip work:** Add 2 min of 90/90 switches after any double-day (AM run + PM strength)
- **Sleep:** Stretching before bed improves sleep quality during deficit — do the hip flexor + hamstring sequence as a nighttime routine if morning mobility was skipped

## DESK WORKER MINI-BREAKS (2 min, every 60-90 min)

1. **Stand + hip circles** — 20s
2. **Shoulder dislocates** (with band or towel) — 10 reps
3. **Standing hip flexor stretch** — 20s/side
4. **Neck circles** — 10s each direction

## INJURY/PAIN TRACKING

Track in daily Status table:

```markdown
| Pain/Tightness | None / [location] [severity 1-5] |
```

**Severity scale:**

- **1** — Awareness only, no impact on movement
- **2** — Mild tightness, warms up and disappears
- **3** — Noticeable during activity, doesn't alter gait
- **4** — Alters movement pattern, requires modification
- **5** — Cannot perform activity, seek medical assessment

**Rules:**

- Severity 1-2: Train normally, note in log
- Severity 3: Reduce volume 30%, avoid aggravating movements
- Severity 4+: Rest that body part, substitute non-aggravating activity
- Same location at severity 3+ for 3 consecutive days: See physiotherapist

**Cut-phase pain escalation:** During deficit phases (Phases 2, 4, 5), any severity 3+ triggers an immediate calorie bump to maintenance (2,300) for that day plus the following day. Deficit-related recovery impairment can mask injury onset. Report to weekly review.
