# Master Plan Synthesis (Pure Concurrent Training)

#### 1. Weekly Training Structure
*This schedule uses Claude's smartest feature: isolating leg day on Thursday with no running, protecting your running mechanics while still driving hypertrophy during the cut.*

*   **Monday:** Easy Base Run (45-60 min, <142 bpm) + Upper Body Push (3-4 sets x 8-12 reps)
*   **Tuesday:** Quality Run 1 (e.g., 6x1km at 10K pace, HR 160-172)
*   **Wednesday:** Easy Base Run (45 min, <142 bpm) + Upper Body Pull (3-4 sets x 8-12 reps)
*   **Thursday:** Leg Day (Strength only. No running. Squats/Hinges at RPE 7-8)
*   **Friday:** Full Rest (Mobility and walking only)
*   **Saturday:** Quality Run 2 (Sub-Threshold / Norwegian intervals, e.g., 8x3 min at 155-165 bpm)
*   **Sunday:** Long Run (75-120 min, <145 bpm) 

*(Note: On double days like Mon/Wed, always run first, then lift 6-8 hours later if possible, or immediately after if schedule demands it. Never lift immediately before running).*

#### 2. Periodization Overview (12-Week Macro View)
*   **Weeks 1-4 (Speed Reintro & Cut):** High volume cut. Calories at 1,850 avg. Running volume 35-40 km. Hypertrophy focus in the gym.
*   **Weeks 5-8 (Peak Cut & Maintenance Transition):** Deepest cut (1,800 avg) for weeks 5-6. **Week 7 is a mandatory Diet Break** at maintenance (2,300 cal) to reset metabolic adaptation and lower cortisol. Volume 45 km.
*   **Weeks 9-11 (10K Peak + Reverse Diet):** Calories scale up (+100/week) to fuel performance. Leg lifting volume drops to just 1 maintenance set by week 10. Quality intervals lengthen (e.g., 4x1.5km).
*   **Week 12 (Race Week):** Gym volume drops to light "pump" upper-body only by Wednesday. Taper running volume to ~15km. Carb load begins T-72h.

#### 3. Daily Nutrition Framework
*Baseline stats: ~76.8kg, Target 70-72kg. Eating Window: 12:30–21:00.*
*   **Protein:** Locked at 160-165g daily. (Crucial for muscle retention in a cut).
*   **Fats:** Locked at 55-60g daily. (Crucial for hormonal baseline).
*   **Carbs/Calories:** Scaled strictly by Day Type:
    *   *Rest Days (Friday):* 1,600 kcal (Carbs ~100g)
    *   *Easy / Gym Only Days (Mon/Wed/Thu):* 1,750 kcal (Carbs ~140g)
    *   *Quality / Long Run Days (Tue/Sat/Sun):* 2,100 kcal (Carbs ~225g)
*   **Timing:**
    *   AM (Pre-workout): If run > 60m or Quality: 1 Banana + Black Coffee. <60m Easy run: Fasted.
    *   12:30 (Post-workout window): 35g Whey + Berries.
    *   Main carb loads concentrated around training (pre-run and post-run meals).

#### 4. Race-Day Protocol (10K)
*   **T-48h to T-24h:** Calories strictly at maintenance (~2,300), push carbs to 300g+. Hydrate with 4L water + electrolytes daily.
*   **Race Morning (T-2h):** 60g easy-to-digest carbs (e.g., plain bagel with honey, white rice, or graham crackers). 500ml water.
*   **Race Morning (T-45m):** 230mg Caffeine. Cease liquids to clear bladder.
*   **In-Race:** No fuel needed for a sub-46 min effort. 

#### 5. Sleep-Disruption Decision Tree (The "Automated Stop-Loss")
*With a newborn, motivation is a liar. Let the data make the decisions.*

*   **If Sleep < 5.5 hours OR deep sleep < 45 mins:**
    *   *Intervention:* Convert ANY planned threshold/interval run to a purely Zone 2 Easy run. Cap gym work at 2 sets per exercise instead of 4.
*   **If Sleep Score < 60 for 2 consecutive nights:**
    *   *Intervention:* Skip the gym entirely. Cut running volume by 30%. Keep intensity (intervals) but reduce the number of reps. 
*   **If Garmin Body Battery is < 30 upon waking:**
    *   *Intervention:* Full day of absolute rest. Sleep debt is non-negotiable.

#### 6. Adaptation Triggers
*   **When to Back Off (Overtraining signs):** 
    *   Morning Resting Heart Rate (RHR) is elevated > 5 bpm for 3 straight days.
    *   Running HR drift > 10% on an easy run (e.g., you normally sit at 135 bpm at 6:30/km, but today you drift to 150 bpm by minute 40 at the same pace).
    *   Gym strength drops > 10% across 2 consecutive sessions (drop volume, not weight).
*   **When to Progress:**
    *   Paces at your 142 bpm MAF cap drop by > 5 seconds/km consistently for a week.

#### 7. Evidence-Backed Supplements
*   **Creatine Monohydrate:** 5g daily (take anytime). *Crucial for retaining intramuscular water and strength during the caloric deficit.*
*   **Caffeine:** 200-250mg strategically *only* before quality runs or races. Hard stop at 13:00 to protect sleep architecture.
*   **Magnesium Glycinate or Taurate:** 400mg taken 45 mins before bed. *Nervous system down-regulator post-training/parenting.*
*   **Vitamin D3 + K2:** 4,000-5,000 IU daily with dietary fat (morning or lunch). *Bone health and immune support.*
*   **Collagen Peptides + 50mg Vitamin C:** 15g taken 45-60 mins prior to running. *Prevents Achilles/patellar tendon stiffness during high running volume blocks.*
