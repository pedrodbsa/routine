## Meal Distribution

**Eating window:** 12:30-21:00. Adjust only if adherence suffers.

### Standard Day (Rest / Easy)

| Meal               | Time  | Notes                               |
| ------------------ | ----- | ----------------------------------- |
| Post-workout shake | 12:30 | 35 g whey + 1 banana + creatine 5 g |
| Lunch              | 13:00 | Protein + carb + veg + fat          |
| Snack              | 17:00 | Protein + small carb/fat            |
| Dinner             | 21:00 | Protein + carb + veg                |

**Portion scaling:** Protein and fat stay mostly constant. Carbs move up or down with day type.

### Quality / Long Run Day

Add roughly 300 calories, mostly carbs:

- Pre-run banana: +100 cal
- Post-run shake: add 40 g oats, +120 cal
- Lunch: extra 30 g rice or equivalent, +75 cal

## Tracking & Adjustments

**Daily:** Log food in Cronometer, morning Withings weigh-in, confirm protein floor.

**Weekly (Sunday):** Compute 7-day weight average, 7-day calorie average, and review the Sunday body-comp scan for trend direction.

| Signal                                  | Action                                    |
| --------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| 7-day weight average stalls 2+ weeks    | Reduce daily target by 100 cal from carbs |
| 7-day weight average drops >0.8 kg/week | Add 100 cal, carbs first                  |
| Motivation <2 for 3+ days               | Insert unplanned refeed day at 2,300 cal  |
| Lean mass declines 2+ weeks             | Raise calories 100-200, carbs first       |
| 3+ untracked days in a week             | Flag in weekly report and audit honestly  |
| Low motivation 5+ days                  | Consider moving diet break earlier        |

## Race Week Nutrition

### 10K (Oct 25 - A-Race)

- Week before: 2,300-2,400 cal, carbs 250 g+
- Final 3 days: carb load to 300 g+, reduce fiber, use familiar foods
- Night before: moderate carbs, low fiber, early dinner
- Race morning: 50-80 g easy carbs, 500 ml water, caffeine 230 mg 30-60 min pre-race
- No in-race fueling needed

### HM (Historical Reference)

- Keep the HM fueling pattern as a reference only for future longer races.
