### Easy Run Guidelines

| Environment | MAF Ceiling | Spike Max | Typical Pace |
| ----------- | ----------- | --------- | ------------ |
| Treadmill   | 142 bpm     | 145 bpm   | 6:40-7:30/km |
| Outdoor     | 148 bpm     | 152 bpm   | 6:30-7:30/km |

- Easy = RPE 3-4 and full conversation.
- If RPE exceeds 5, slow down even if HR looks acceptable.
- Recovery jogs sit below 133 bpm.

## Workout Types

### Easy/Base Runs

- **HR:** 128-142 treadmill, 128-148 outdoor
- **Pace:** 6:15-7:30/km
- **Duration:** 30-60 min
- **RPE:** 3-4

### Long Runs

- **HR:** 128-145 with drift ceiling around 150 outdoor when prescribed
- **Duration:** 75-120 min depending on phase
- **RPE:** 4-5
- Long runs over 90 minutes use quality-day fueling.

### Sub-Threshold Intervals

| Session          | Structure        | HR Target                           | Recovery      |
| ---------------- | ---------------- | ----------------------------------- | ------------- |
| Norwegian Long   | 3x10 to 4x10 min | 145-152 treadmill / 148-160 outdoor | 60-90 sec jog |
| Norwegian Short  | 8-10x3 min       | 148-155 treadmill / 152-165 outdoor | 45-60 sec jog |
| Cruise Intervals | 5-6x1 km         | 150-165 outdoor                     | 60-90 sec jog |
| HM-pace          | 4-6x2 km         | Goal HM effort                      | 60 sec jog    |

### Tempo

- **HR:** 155-168
- **Structure:** 20-30 min continuous
- **RPE:** 7-8

### 10K-Pace Intervals

- **HR:** 160-172
- **Pace:** 4:36-4:50/km
- **Structure:** 6-8x1 km or 4-5x1.5 km at goal 10K pace, 60 sec jog recovery
- **RPE:** 8-9
- Introduce from Cut Block 1 (May 26). This becomes the core 10K-specific session.

### VO2max Intervals

- **HR:** 165-180 during work reps
- **Pace:** faster than 10K pace, typically 800 m to 1 km repeats
- **RPE:** 9-10
- Reserve for late Cut Block 1 and Phase 6 when calories support it.

### X-Element

- Hill sprints: 6-8x10-15 sec
- Strides: 4-8x20 sec after easy runs
- Short reps: 6-8x200 m at mile effort

## Weekly Rules By Phase

- **Phase 1:** Easy-only running. No quality work.
- **Phase 2:** Tuesday is the only quality day. Thursday is always no-running.
- **Phase 3:** Race block uses sharpeners, no heavy taper.
- **Phases 4-5:** Tuesday and Friday quality, Thursday no-running, Sunday long run.
- **Phase 6:** Same Tuesday/Friday quality rhythm, but add VO2max and taper volume over the final 3 weeks.

## Monitoring

### RPE Scale

| RPE  | Meaning   | Use                  |
| ---- | --------- | -------------------- |
| 3-4  | Easy      | Easy runs, recovery  |
| 5-6  | Moderate  | Avoid on easy days   |
| 7-8  | Hard      | Tempo, sub-threshold |
| 9-10 | Very hard | VO2max, race kick    |

### ACWR

Acute km this week / 4-week rolling average.

| ACWR    | Status          | Action          |
| ------- | --------------- | --------------- |
| 0.8-1.3 | Sweet spot      | Continue        |
| 1.3-1.5 | Caution         | Monitor fatigue |
| >1.5    | Danger          | Reduce volume   |
| <0.8    | Detraining risk | Build gradually |

### Warning Signs

- Easy-run HR elevated 5+ bpm above baseline for 3 runs -> extra rest day
- Sleep score <60 for 2 nights -> replace quality with easy
- RHR elevated 5+ bpm above baseline -> reduce weekly volume 20%
- HRV drops >15% from 7-day average -> optional rest day or reduce quality
- Body Battery <30 at wake -> full rest day
