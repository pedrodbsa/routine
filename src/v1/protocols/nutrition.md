# NUTRITION PROTOCOL

## DAILY TARGETS (Race Prep — Fat Loss Phase)

| Macro         | Ratio | Grams | Calories  |
| ------------- | ----- | ----- | --------- |
| Protein       | 33%   | 156g  | 624       |
| Carbohydrates | 40%   | 190g  | 760       |
| Fat           | 27%   | 57g   | 513       |
| **Total**     |       |       | **1,900** |

**Rationale:** ~400-500 cal deficit for ~0.4 kg/week fat loss. Protein at 2.0 g/kg preserves muscle. Carbs at 190g fuels 35-45 km/week running (min 3-5 g/kg for endurance in deficit). Previous 1,787 cal was too aggressive — January showed 67% lean mass loss.

**High-intensity days (long runs, tempo, intervals):** Add ~300 cal (mostly carbs) → **2,200 cal** (~200 cal deficit).

---

## PERFORMANCE FUELING

| Run Type        | Pre-Run              | Timing          |
| --------------- | -------------------- | --------------- |
| Easy/Base <75min | **FASTED**          | Water/coffee only |
| Long Run >90min | 1 banana + coffee    | 30 min before   |
| Tempo/Intervals | Light snack if needed | 30 min before  |
| Race            | 50-80g easy carbs    | 2-3h before     |

Fasted easy runs train fat oxidation. High-intensity fasted elevates cortisol excessively — always fuel quality sessions.

**Eating window:** 12:30-21:00 (IF). Post-workout shake within 30 min of training (MPS window most responsive 0-2h).

**High-intensity day additions:** Pre-run banana (+100 cal), 40g oats in shake (+120 cal), extra 30g rice at lunch (+75 cal) → ~2,200 cal total.

## MEAL OPTIONS

### Post-Workout Shake (280 cal)
35g whey + 1 banana + 5g creatine + 250ml water

### Lunch (~550 cal)
- **Chicken & Rice:** 180g chicken, 120g rice, 200g veg, 1 tbsp olive oil (525 cal, 44P/38C/15F)
- **Salmon & Potatoes:** 150g salmon, 150g potatoes, 200g veg (460 cal, 37P/32C/16F)
- **Lean Beef Bowl:** 150g beef, 100g rice, 200g veg, 1 tbsp olive oil (500 cal, 34P/30C/16F)
- **Eggs & Toast:** 4 eggs, 2 slices whole grain, 150g veg (480 cal, 30P/34C/21F)

### Snack (~250 cal)
- **Greek Yogurt:** 200g 0%, 15g almonds, 50g berries (245 cal, 25P/12C/9F)
- **Protein Shake:** 25g whey, 1 apple, 10g almond butter (240 cal, 24P/22C/6F)
- **Cottage Cheese:** 200g 2%, 100g fruit (210 cal, 28P/15C/4F)

### Dinner (~650 cal)
- **Steak & Veg:** 180g sirloin, 150g potatoes, 250g veg, 1 tsp oil (560 cal, 48P/34C/18F)
- **Chicken Stir-Fry:** 200g chicken, 100g rice, 300g veg, 1 tbsp coconut oil (550 cal, 48P/32C/15F)
- **Fish & Quinoa:** 180g white fish, 150g quinoa, 250g veg, 1 tbsp oil (530 cal, 44P/34C/15F)
- **Turkey Meatballs:** 200g turkey, 100g pasta, 100g tomato sauce, 200g veg (580 cal, 46P/32C/16F)

All dinner portions: 180g protein source, 150g carb source, 250g veg, 1 tsp olive oil.

## CAFFEINE

- **Intake:** 2-4 cups/day
- **Hard cutoff:** 15:00 (9h before sleep target)
- **Race day:** 3mg/kg (~230mg) 30-60 min pre-race
- **If sleep drops:** Tighten to 13:00 for one week

---

## HYDRATION

3.5-4L daily. 500ml pre-workout, 400-500ml with each meal, 500ml post-workout.

---

## TRACKING & ADJUSTMENTS

**Daily:** Log food in Cronometer, morning weigh-in (post-bathroom, pre-food), confirm 156g+ protein.

**Weekly (Sunday):** 7-day weight average, calorie adherence review, compare to prior week.

| Situation                | Action                                |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------- |
| Weight up 2 weeks        | Cut 100 cal (reduce rice 50g/meal)    |
| Weight down >0.8 kg/week | Add 100 cal                          |
| Energy crashes mid-run   | Add 50g carbs around training         |
| Protein under 150g       | Extra egg whites or shake             |
| Strength dropping        | Raise protein to 170g, add pre-WO carbs |
| Lean mass declining      | Raise cals 100-200, carbs first       |
| Stall 3+ weeks           | Audit tracking → drop to 1,700 2wk → add 20 min walking → diet break at 2,300 1wk |

## RACE WEEK NUTRITION

- **Week before:** Maintenance 2,200 cal, carbs 200g+, reduce fiber
- **Night before:** Familiar foods, moderate carbs, low fiber, early dinner (19-20h)
- **Race morning:** Light breakfast 2-3h before — 50-80g easy carbs, 500ml water, nothing new
