The athlete will **not** train calves in any form (stated 2026-06-30, extended 2026-09-29). His calves are large because of low insertions (long muscle bellies), his legs are bulky, and he wants to tone them down, not add size.

**Why:** aesthetic — leg and calf size, not function. Parallels the standing "legs are not a hypertrophy target" call and [[feedback-no-box-jumps]], [[feedback-run-over-legs-legs-bulky]].

**How to apply:**
- No calf raises anywhere: standing, seated, loaded, bodyweight or soleus, in Legs, accessories, the daily mobility routine or the runner prehab microdose. Calf *stretches* are fine.
- The Achilles/calf-stiffness role is carried by running and the plyo block / quality-day power drill (elastic loading, minimal hypertrophy).
- The calf rows in `strength-exercises.md` are kept for enums only; do not select them.
- Be straight about expectations: calf size is mostly genetic and running at 35–45 km/wk maintains it. Legs slim through fat loss and the absence of leg hypertrophy work, not through any exercise choice — do not promise more.
