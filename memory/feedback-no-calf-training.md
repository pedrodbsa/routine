The athlete will **not** train calves directly (stated 2026-06-30). His calves are already large and disproportionate to the rest of his body, and he does not want them bigger.

**Why:** aesthetic — calf size, not function. Heavy-slow calf raises drive real calf hypertrophy, which he is explicitly avoiding. Parallels the standing "legs are not a hypertrophy target" call and [[feedback-no-box-jumps]].

**How to apply:** never put calf raises (standing/seated, DB/BB/machine) in any leg or accessory session. The leg-day calf slot has been removed from `training.md`. The run-relevant Achilles/calf-stiffness benefit that slot served is retained through the plyometric block (jumps / A-skips — elastic Achilles loading, minimal hypertrophy) plus running's own calf load — so the tendon rationale isn't lost, only the hypertrophy stimulus. The calf rows in `strength-exercises.md` are kept for history/enums only; do not select them.
