On 2026-10-19 the athlete said: "I have a tendency to gain weight - sometimes I snack or I have alcohol. I think I was feeling better under the cut." He went from ~74 to ~76 kg within weeks of the reverse and build tiers, and found it very frustrating that months of cutting reverted so quickly. He wants to reach 70 kg and thinks "staying on a light deficit is the way to achieve that and to make me more committed."

**Why:** August's Phase 5 tiers (~2,050 kcal reference-week average) held weight flat with social evenings included, so that is his effective maintenance. The +300 build tiers (~2,340) are a real surplus for him, not glycogen alone. The consult's ~2,450 maintenance estimate was optimistic.

**How to apply:** when choosing tiers, treat ~2,050 plan-day average as maintenance unless fasted data says otherwise. A deficit has to come from social and snack control, not from cutting plan days he already follows exactly. Don't propose a surplus phase without naming the regain risk.
