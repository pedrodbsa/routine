The athlete rejected a coach-designed split that spread the small muscles across the three sessions: "dont liek that msucle split. I want major+minor. we have 3 majors: chest, back, legs. and 3 minors: bicep, tricep and shoulders. (abs are part of every seession)". He had used chest + biceps and chest + triceps before and never settled which pairing was better.

The pairing chosen: **Chest + biceps, Back + triceps, Legs + shoulders.** Each arm muscle goes with the opposite major, so it is fresh for its direct work and gets a second, indirect hit 48 h later (rows for biceps, bench and dips for triceps). Shoulders go with legs, which keeps the overhead press away from the bench day.

**Why:** the structure is his, and a split he likes is one he delivers. The pairing inside it was the coach's call.

**How to apply:** keep the one-major-plus-one-minor shape and abs in every session. Adjust volume inside the slots, and never move a minor across sessions or add a fourth session unless he asks. If he wants to try synergist pairing (chest + triceps, back + biceps), that is a legitimate swap, since the evidence difference is small. Templates: `protocols/training.md`.
