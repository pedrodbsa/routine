When choosing the week's harder quality run, derive it from the periodization ladder (sub-T → threshold → 10K-pace → VO2) and the current phase week — not from the athlete's outcome words or the prior day's framing.

On 2026-06-16 (Cut Block 1 wk3) I prescribed a 10K-pace 5×1km because the athlete said he wanted "speed work" and the day had already been framed as "10K-pace sharpening." That pulled a wk4 session forward a week. The athlete surfaced Garmin's Daily Suggested Workout — a 22-min threshold tempo — which was the correctly-sequenced next rung (he'd done only sub-T: Norwegian + cruise). I agreed it was better. The miss was real: I optimized off his phrasing and path-dependence instead of the training model.

**Why:** quality selection must come from the periodization model, not from the athlete's wording or yesterday's plan. Pulling an intensity rung forward without an explicit reason skips the threshold base that 10K-pace work sits on and raises overcook risk. The athlete trusts coaching autonomy and expects me to re-derive, not just execute his phrasing — see [[coaching-lessons]].

**How to apply:** (1) Before prescribing the harder quality, name the current rung on the ladder + the phase week, and pick the next correct rung. (2) Present the 1–2 best session options with their periodization rationale rather than collapsing to one. (3) Cross-check against Garmin's Daily Suggested Workout; when it diverges from mine, reconcile explicitly (it's an independent signal). (4) Any rung pulled forward needs an explicit reason logged in the daily file. (5) Whatever the session, keep HR/effort the governor over locked pace in heat — see [[feedback-pace-hr-calibration]].
