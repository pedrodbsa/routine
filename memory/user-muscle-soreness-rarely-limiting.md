Told on 2026-10-26: legs were a 2–3/10 the day after the Oct 25 10K (avg HR 178), and soreness "is rarely a problem for me". He was making a general point, not proposing to run that day (a misread I made and he corrected).

**Why it matters:** downhill running and hill work are not limited by quad soreness for him, and the soreness caution I gave on rolling long runs was unnecessary. The limiting recovery signals are the ones Garmin shows: resting HR, HRV, sleep and Body Battery (see [[feedback-soft-markers-check-anxiety]] for the other direction).

**How to apply:**
- Do not cap downhill or rolling-terrain volume on soreness grounds. Keep the rule against long hard downhill repeats only for the leg-size reason in [[feedback-run-over-legs-legs-bulky]], not for soreness.
- Do not take "legs feel fine" as a green light. After a hard effort the post-race and readiness rules still read RHR, HRV and sleep.
- A soreness question in `/plan` is a check, not a gate.
