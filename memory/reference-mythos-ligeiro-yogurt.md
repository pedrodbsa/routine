Athlete's habitual Greek yogurt: **Iogurte Grego Mythos Ligeiro Natural — Continente Equilíbrio**.

Per 100 g (label, confirmed 2026-06-20): **55 kcal · protein 5.3 g · fat 2.0 g (sat 1.4) · carbs 3.9 g (all sugars) · fiber 0 · salt 0.14 g.**

Non-obvious point for meal planning: despite being "Greek yogurt," the *ligeiro* (light) version is **protein-weak — 5.3 g/100 g, roughly half a Fage 0% / skyr (~10 g).** A normal 200–250 g serving only delivers ~11–13 g protein, so it will **not** carry the ≥165 g protein floor or the ≥40 g pre-bed feed on its own. When using it as the protein anchor, add ~20 g whey (or use ~400 g+) to clear the floor. The fat is also low (2 g/100 g), so it adds little satiety — pair with nuts/PB when the day's fat is short. See [[feedback-tracking-adherence]].

**Better swaps the athlete will buy (decided 2026-06-21):** athlete **dislikes skyr texture** and finds Fage hard to source locally. Preferred creamy high-protein replacement = **Danone YoPRO Big Pot Natural (10 g P/100 g, 0% fat, 58 kcal)** — smooth/creamy, widely stocked (Continente/Pingo Doce/Jumbo); 250 g = ~25 g protein, clears the pre-bed feed with no whey. Cheaper own-brand fallback = **Continente Iogurte Proteico (7.2 g P/100 g)**. Shelf rule: ignore "grego/ligeiro" branding, buy on the protein number (target ≥9–10 g/100 g).
