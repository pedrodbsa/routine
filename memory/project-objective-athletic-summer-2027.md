At the first `/consult` (2026-09-29) the athlete retired the Dec 12 A-race: "the race became less important to me. The December race will actually be a leisure race with friends. My goal is to get in a better shape … I want to get to next summer in the best shape possible." Shape means *athletic* — "not very bulky but with a good amount of muscle and low body fat … no need for the Brad Pitt Fight Club look, I'm 40 now" — plus faster running, "ideally a 45 min 10K."

**Standing decisions (full sheet in `logbook/2026-09/consult-2026-09-29.md`):**

- **Priority: body composition > running fitness > racing. Sleep onset governs.** Tie-breaks follow this order — a single slot goes by the drop order, builds carry a weight ceiling, no deficit is blocked by a race except the spring A-race window.
- **Scorecard, Aug 1 2027:** ~15% BF → ~70–71.5 kg (lean ≥59.6 kg, loads as proxy — no Bod Pod re-test, athlete's call); bench 60×8 / OHP 35×8 / lat pulldown 110×12×4 (the G1 stack tops out at 110) / bench dip +25×10 / 5 strict pull-ups (from ~1; audit follow-up 2026-09-29); weights are fasted home-scale readings; **sub-47 10K in spring 2027** (race TBD by Feb 1); **45:00 is a stretch for autumn 2027**, not before (needs LT2 pace 5:00 → ~4:35; ~4 kg of weight loss buys ~2–3%, the rest is a year of 40+ km weeks).
- **Phases:** Reverse (Sep 21–Oct 11) → **Build Oct 12–Jan 4** (+300 plan-day tiers, band 74.5–75.5, ceiling 76.0 / floor 73.5) → **Cut 3 Jan 5–Apr 12** (0.3 kg/wk, tiers derived Jan 4 as build tiers − 330 − the build's weight drift) → maintenance + spring race Apr 13–Jun 14 → optional lean-out Jun 15–Jul 26. Path chosen because the athlete eats more in the cold months — maintenance goes where appetite is high, the deficit into Jan–Apr. Rejected: cut again now, mini-cut to Dec, single 20-week cut.
- **Races:** Oct 25 hilly 10K = B, hard effort by HR (~172–176), recorded as a read. Dec 12 = social; mode (group pace vs solo hard) decided by Dec 5. No HM/trail before June 2027 ("likely to change" — revisit Jan 4).
- **Capacity (athlete-stated):** 4–5 runs + 3 lifts/week, trips of 90+ min possible, full capacity from Oct 6; trekking Nov 4–8.

**Why:** every rule in the stack from Jul 31 to Sep 29 was built to protect a race the athlete no longer cares about, and the cut it was protecting had already stalled for seven weeks with no strength stimulus. The new order makes strength survive compressed weeks and puts the deficit where it can work.

**How to apply:** plan from this objective, not from the archived Dec 12 A-race plan (`protocols/archive/running-dec12-arace-plan.md`).
