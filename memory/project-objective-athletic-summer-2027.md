At the first `/consult` (2026-09-29) the athlete retired the Dec 12 A-race: "the race became less important to me. The December race will actually be a leisure race with friends. My goal is to get in a better shape … I want to get to next summer in the best shape possible." Shape means *athletic* — "not very bulky but with a good amount of muscle and low body fat … no need for the Brad Pitt Fight Club look, I'm 40 now" — plus faster running, "ideally a 45 min 10K."

**Standing decisions (full sheet in `logbook/2026-09/consult-2026-09-29.md`):**

- **Priority: body composition > running fitness > racing. Sleep onset governs.** Tie-breaks follow this order — a single slot goes by the drop order, the deficit runs Oct 20 – Apr 12 without pausing for B-races, and the spring A-race is run at maintenance.
- **Scorecard, Aug 1 2027:** ~15% BF → ~70–71.5 kg (lean ≥59.6 kg, loads as proxy — no Bod Pod re-test, athlete's call); bench 60×8 / OHP 35×8 / lat pulldown 110×12×4 (the G1 stack tops out at 110) / bench dip +25×10 / 5 strict pull-ups (from ~1; audit follow-up 2026-09-29); weights are fasted home-scale readings; **sub-47 10K in spring 2027** (race TBD by Feb 1); **45:00 is a stretch for autumn 2027**, not before (needs LT2 pace 5:00 → ~4:35; ~4 kg of weight loss buys ~2–3%, the rest is a year of 40+ km weeks).
- **Phases (superseded 2026-10-19):** the Sep 29 path (Build Oct 12 – Jan 4 at +300, then Cut 3 Jan 5 – Apr 12 at 0.3 kg/wk) lasted one week. **The 2026-10-19 consult (`logbook/2026-10/consult-2026-10-19.md`) replaced it with a Phase 8 slow cut, Oct 20 – Apr 12, at 0.2 kg/wk on the Phase 5 tiers** (Rest 1,850 / Easy-Strength 1,950 / Quality 2,250 / Long 2,450, P170 / F60), target ~70.0–70.5 kg, then maintenance + spring race Apr 13 – Jun 14 → optional lean-out. Why: 74 → ~75.7 kg in three weeks on the build tiers, which turned out to be a surplus; see [[user-regains-fast-prefers-light-deficit]]. The Sep 29 rationale ("maintenance where appetite is high") failed on the athlete's own data.
- **Scorecard weight amended 2026-10-19:** ~70.0–70.5 kg (lean held at 59.6; no lean gain assumed in a deficit).
- **Races:** Oct 25 hilly 10K = B, hard effort by HR (~172–176), recorded as a read. Dec 12 = B, run hard with friends (athlete, audit review 2026-09-29: "i'm gonna run hard anyway"); replaces that week's quality and long run, recorded as the last fitness read before the cut. No HM/trail before June 2027 ("likely to change" — revisit Jan 4).
- **Capacity (athlete-stated):** 4–5 runs + 3 lifts/week, trips of 90+ min possible, full capacity from Oct 6; trekking Nov 4–8.

**Why:** every rule in the stack from Jul 31 to Sep 29 was built to protect a race the athlete no longer cares about, and the cut it was protecting had already stalled for seven weeks with no strength stimulus. The new order makes strength survive compressed weeks and puts the deficit where it can work.

**How to apply:** plan from this objective, not from the archived Dec 12 A-race plan (`protocols/archive/running-dec12-arace-plan.md`). Keep the running numbers visible every week regardless — see [[user-motivation-running-numbers]].
