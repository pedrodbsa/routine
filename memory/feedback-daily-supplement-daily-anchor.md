Two linked lessons from the 2026-07-29 creatine finding.

**1. A daily supplement must anchor to something that happens every day.** `supplements.md` said to take the 5 g creatine "with whichever shake is convenient." The post-workout shake gets dropped or shrunk constantly (Jun 25, Jul 20, Jul 22, Jul 27, Jul 28 all logged in 2026), and each time the dose survived only if the athlete remembered to relocate it. Result: intake fell away from the Jul 6 holiday and stayed away 23 days without anyone noticing. **This is structurally identical to the isotretinoin dose hung on dinner** (see [[project-isotretinoin-course]]), which has missed doses three weeks running. Anchor daily things to the morning coffee or another seven-day fixture, never to a training-contingent feed.

**Refinement, 2026-08-04 — "the morning coffee" was not enough.** The dose was re-anchored to the pre-run banana-and-coffee feed and slipped again on the very next quality day: the run started 45 min late, the whole morning shifted with it, and the creatine was missed at its feed (caught and taken ~40 min later, so no dose lost). A feed whose *clock time* floats with the session is still contingent, even when the feed itself happens daily. The anchor has to be an event that does not move — waking, brushing teeth, the first drink of the day — not "the morning meal."

**2. Scale BIA "muscle mass" tracks intracellular water, which is exactly what creatine fills.** A creatine washout drops that channel (observed: 51.61 → 51.28 kg over 23 days off); a reload lifts it. **It is therefore useless as a lean-mass signal for 4–6 weeks either side of a creatine change** — it was set as an early-restore tripwire on the Aug 4 calorie checkpoint and would have fired falsely. Use **strength working loads across consecutive same-type sessions** instead.

**Why:** creatine holds ~0.5–1.5 kg of intracellular water at a chronic 5 g/day dose (the 1–2 kg figures are from 20 g/day loading), washing out on a ~4–6 week half-life. Both the scale weight and the BIA lean channel move with it, in the same direction, for reasons that have nothing to do with fat or muscle.

**How to apply:** when the athlete proposes creatine as an explanation for a weight move, run the washout curve against the actual measurement windows before agreeing — if the baseline window was already weeks into the washout, most of the effect is already priced into the baseline and the window-to-window contribution is small. On restart, warn ahead of time that 0.5–1.5 kg is coming back over 2–4 weeks and is water, so it is not misread as a stall. See [[feedback-weight-drop-water-vs-tissue]].
