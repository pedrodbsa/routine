Plain oats are not tasty to the athlete; mixing fruit into the bowl makes them acceptable (stated 2026-06-21). **The fruit is mango or strawberries — one of the two, always** (stated 2026-07-30). He keeps both frozen, and stirring them in **cools the oatmeal**, which is the real driver on top of palatability. Never default an oat breakfast to banana, apple, or generic "berries".

**Why:** A meal the athlete dislikes is a meal he won't adhere to, and a bowl too hot to eat is a bowl he waits on — which pushes a pre-workout feed late. Palatability is an adherence lever, same as [[feedback-prefers-activity-over-food-cuts]] and [[feedback-fruit-after-lunch-craving-control]]. Other fruit is fine post-lunch or post-dinner, just not in the oat bowl.

**How to apply:** Cut the dry oats by ~15–20 g and add mango or strawberries matching the removed carbs (~20 g dry oats ≈ 13 g carb ≈ ~100 g strawberries ≈ ~85 g mango), so calories and the day-type carb total stay on target. Protein/fat anchors unchanged. Mango is ~2× the carb density of strawberries (15 g vs 8 g per 100 g) — not gram-for-gram interchangeable. Documented in `protocols/meal-rotation.md` § Breakfast Oats — Fruit Mix-In.
