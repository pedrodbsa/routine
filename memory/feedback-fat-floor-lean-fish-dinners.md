Any meal whose planned fat sits on the plate as a pourable oil loses that fat. Observed Jul 1 (51 g), Jul 2 (42 g), Jul 27, and **Jul 29 — where it was the *lunch* oil, not dinner's** (logged chicken + rice + beans + broccoli with the planned 1 tsp simply absent, leaving the day at exactly 60 g before dinner was rebuilt). **Generalize past lean fish: the predictor is not the protein, it is whether the fat is pourable.** Lean white fish (sea bass/robalo, oven-dry dourada) and simple oven/boiled plates just happen to be where pourable oil is the only fat.

**Second failure mode, found Jul 30 (miss #5) — the meal-swap.** Lunch was planned with its fat built in exactly as this memory prescribes (40 g avocado + 1 tsp pan oil, 16 g F). It still missed, landing at 9 g, because the athlete **replaced the whole meal** with a convenience plate (pita + chicken + tzatziki drizzle + watermelon) rather than dropping the garnish. Building fat into one *ingredient* protects it against the garnish being skipped, but not against the dish being swapped — and swaps are frequent, since meals get grabbed on the move.

**Why:** Fat is the one macro with a hard floor (hormonal health + isotretinoin context). A droppable garnish is not a reliable floor — it's the first thing to go. The athlete prefers activity over eating more (see [[feedback-prefers-activity-over-food-cuts]]), so the fix is not "add calories" — it's making the planned fat non-optional within the meal.

**How to apply:** Never plan a meal whose fat is a removable drizzle — on any meal, program the fat as an integral part of the dish — cook the fish in olive oil, or add olives / avocado / a fattier side / nuts. **Then assume one meal per day gets swapped anyway: don't leave the day's fat floor dependent on any single meal surviving.** Bank the majority of the 60 g in the meals eaten at home under least time pressure (breakfast eggs, dinner), so a grabbed lunch can land at 9 g without breaking the day. On `/log`, re-tune dinner's fat *up* the moment a lunch logs light rather than waiting to discover the shortfall at 20:00. Iso absorption itself only needs one fat-containing meal; this is about the daily 60 g floor, tracked separately. See the 2026-07-02 and 2026-07-30 daily-file Actuals flags.
