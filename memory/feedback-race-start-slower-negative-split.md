On race morning, 2026-10-25, the athlete asked to open at ~5:10 and speed up late: "last time I started way too fast and I noticed the race got affected by that early 4:30 pace." He ran km 1 at 5:16 (HR 169), the middle at ~4:55–5:09, and closed 4:49 → 4:50 → 4:42 → 4:33. The 10 km lap came in at 49:27, a PR by ~30 s over the 49:57 of Mar 8, on an avg HR of 178. The halves were 25:24 and 24:03.

**Why:** His failure mode in races is the start, not the finish. Adrenaline puts km 1 at ~4:30 when the race pace is ~5:00. A deliberately slow first km costs ~10 s and buys the closing kilometres back several times over.

**How to apply:** In every race strategy, open km 1 about 10 s/km slower than goal pace with HR ≤170, run goal pace through the middle, and close from km 8. Expect an average HR of ~176–180 for a full 10K effort: the old 172–176 band under-states it. A PacePro plan built on the course should show a slow first two km. Pair this with [[feedback-pace-hr-calibration]] for the goal pace, and with [[feedback-sedative-confounds-hrv-rhr]] for reading the gate on race morning.
