The athlete moved his A-race from Oct 25 (hilly, no PR possible — demoted to a B-race hard supported effort) to **Dec 12** in a conversation, but the decision was never written to the files. Every subsequent session — including a full protocol audit — kept planning against the stale Oct 25 calendar, and one even added a "post-Oct cut" that collided head-on with the Dec A-race build. Discovered and fixed 2026-07-31 (calendar restructure + archive).

**Why:** the stack, not the conversation, is the coach's memory. A decision that only exists in chat is a decision the next session will silently contradict; the athlete then finds the protocols "all over the place" and trust erodes.

**How to apply:** whenever a race date, goal, phase boundary, or target changes in conversation — even casually — write it into `current-status.md` (coaching log + affected tables) and the touched protocol files **before the session ends**, and update the race-calendar line in [[MEMORY]]. If the athlete references a past decision that has no trace in the stack, say plainly that it is unrecorded and reconstruct it with him rather than pretending to remember it. Related: [[feedback-rederive-not-inherit]].
