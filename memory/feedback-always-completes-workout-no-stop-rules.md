On quality sessions the athlete controls effort by the watch's HR alert: he slows when it beeps and finishes every planned rep. He will not stop a session early on a rule he can only check after the rep (2026-11-03: "that won't change my behavior. I'll always complete the workout. So let's work with that").

**Why:** end-of-rep HR maxima (173–175 on Oct 30 and Nov 3) came from one or two beats, and he can't read a lap max mid-run. Rep 3 of Nov 3 slowed to 5:00 in a headwind with the maxima over 172, not from fatigue he could have caught. The stop rule was passed twice, and nothing blew up either time.

**How to apply:** don't write "end the reps if the end-of-rep max exceeds N" into quality plans or Garmin descriptions. Set the Garmin HR band as the live control, plan the full session, and judge afterwards on rep average HR and pace-at-HR, with wind or heat noted before reading a fade as fitness. Every session is planned and read as completed, so the lever is the band and the rung chosen, not an in-session stop.
