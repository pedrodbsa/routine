The athlete's **hardest hunger window is post-dinner / late evening**, not the afternoon. He prefers a **smaller midafternoon snack** in exchange for a guaranteed **post-dinner snack / dessert** (e.g. skyr + berries + honey). Stated 2026-07-15.

**Why:** meal timing is fat-loss-neutral at fixed daily calories + protein, so moving calories/carbs from the afternoon to a post-dinner dessert costs nothing metabolically and directly manages the window where his adherence is most at risk (evening grazing). This is the same adherence-lever logic that reinstated the midafternoon snack on 2026-06-22 — but for *him* the binding time is the evening, so the anchor shifts later.

**How to apply:** on `/plan` and `/log`, keep the afternoon snack lean/small (skyr + ½ whey, ~30 g protein, low cal) and add a **post-dinner dessert** carrying the reserved carbs. Keep it **soon after dinner, not right before bed** (sleep-onset latency is his live recovery issue — see [[feedback-soft-markers-check-anxiety]]) and keep dinner as the ≥40 g pre-bed protein meal (the dessert is a light carb + small protein feed after it). This partially revises the "midafternoon snack anchors every day" structure in `nutrition.md` § Meal Distribution — flag for `/report` to fold into the protocol. Relates to [[feedback-fruit-after-lunch-craving-control]] (same craving-management theme).
