On Chest + biceps day, the athlete does not want the weighted bench dip in slot 3. Said mid-session on 2026-10-21, after the plan gave 3 × 12–15 at +16 kg. He did the sets that day and asked that it not recur on chest days.

**Why:** the bench dip is mostly triceps, and Back + triceps had loaded triceps two days earlier (pushdown, overhead extension). He understood the slot as a normal dip for the lower pec. The template (`training.md` Chest slot 3, and the "four scorecard lifts are fixed" line) makes it a fixed benchmark, and `/plan` followed it without weighing the overlap.

**How to apply:**
- Next Chest + biceps: leave the weighted bench dip out of the chest slot. Use a chest-biased movement for slot 3 instead (parallel-bar dip leaning forward, or a decline or low-to-high cable press), after checking `strength-exercises.md` for what G1 can do. The plan has treated full dips as not available at G1, so check that first.
- The bench dip is still a scorecard lean-mass marker (target +25 × 10). It needs another home, for example Back + triceps. That is a `protocols/` change, so propose it through `/report` or `/consult`. Do not edit the protocol from a daily plan.
- Flag the overlap when a lift is both a scorecard marker and a triceps-dominant movement placed within 48 h of a triceps day. See [[feedback-verify-load-baseline-against-athlete]] for the same habit of checking a fixed slot against the athlete.
