Before building routes, courses, or any location-specific prescription, **ask for the exact town
or address** — do not infer it from a grouped answer or a regional label in the stack.

**Why:** on 2026-08-13 the stack recorded only "Algarve" for the Aug 15–23 holiday. The athlete was
offered a grouped option "Tavira / Cabanas / east" and picked it; the coach anchored five Garmin
courses on Tavira town, flagged the assumption in writing twice, and shipped them anyway. He is
based in **Cabanas**, so every route started in the wrong town and all five courses were deleted.
Flagging an assumption is not the same as resolving it — the whole deliverable was geographic, so
the one unknown that could invalidate it was worth a direct question.

**How to apply:** when the deliverable depends on a location, make the location question specific
and free-text rather than a menu of regions, and ask it *before* doing the work rather than
alongside it. The same rule holds for gym, accommodation, and race-start specifics. If the athlete
would rather build the routes himself, supply the workouts and let him own the geography — that is
what he chose here.
