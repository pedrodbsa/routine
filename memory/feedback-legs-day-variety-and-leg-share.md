On 2026-10-28 the athlete said the Legs + shoulders session was "way too shoulder heavy, and light on the legs", looked like the same workout as before, and that the stack keeps using the same exercises ("bulgarian split squats, front raises, arnold presses, back extensions, lunges").

**Why:** the day ran 6 leg sets against 12 shoulder sets because the leg half had been halved on Tuesday's soft markers and the file was carried over to a Green Wednesday without re-deriving it ([[feedback-rederive-not-inherit]]). The shoulder slots (OHP fixed, lateral raise and jump squats allowed to repeat) are the ones `training.md` § Exercise Rotation lets repeat. The leg half also lost its optional single-leg slot and the hamstring slot in the last two sessions. The leg spec is deliberately low-volume (legs are not a hypertrophy target, [[feedback-run-over-legs-legs-bulky]]), so "more legs" means variety and slot coverage, not 8–15-rep quad volume.

**How to apply:** when the leg half is not downgraded by a marker, prescribe all four slots (primary, explosive, single-leg prehab, hamstring) and rotate slot 3 (Bulgarian split squat, walking lunge, reverse lunge, step-up) and slot 4 (back extension, Nordic, single-leg RDL) against the last two sessions. Keep shoulders to OHP + one lateral/rear-delt pair rotated across cable and DB variants. Front raises are not in the library; Arnold press is, but cannot replace the fixed OHP scorecard lift. A rotation change in `training.md` goes through `/report`. See [[feedback-strength-rotation-two-session-lookback]].
