Never express eggs in grams in `/plan` or `/log` meal rows — use counts ("3 whole eggs", "3 egg whites"), never "190 g egg whites" or any gram weight for eggs. The athlete has corrected this **three times** (as of 2026-07-12). Grams remain correct for oats, rice, meat, veg, etc. — the rule is specific to eggs.

**Why:** eggs are bought and cracked by count; a gram weight is unusable at the counter and reads as sloppy plan-writing.

**How to apply:** the `meal-rotation.md` breakfast cards (B1 "130 g egg whites", B3 "190 g egg whites", B4) list egg whites in grams — when surfacing them in a plan, convert egg-white grams to a count (~33 g per white) or fold into whole eggs. Watch this on every breakfast row. See [[feedback-oats-fruit-mix-in]], [[feedback-fruit-after-lunch-craving-control]].
