Dermatologist prescribed **isotretinoin** on 2026-06-25, after a long history of recurrent acne that had been modeled in `protocols/skin-log.md` as over-treatment barrier damage (hyp #1) + recurrent localized folliculitis at the nose (glasses-pad spot). The Rx implies a true sebaceous-acne component the gentle barrier approach wouldn't fix — updates the skin-log read (barrier damage was real *and* an underlying acne tendency exists).

**Dose (confirmed 2026-06-25): effectively ~5 mg/day — a MICRODOSE** (athlete is taking ~5 mg/day, adapted from the nominal "10 mg every 3 days" likely due to capsule sizing; ~1/8–1/15 of a standard ~38–76 mg/day course for ~76 kg). Take with a fat-containing meal (fat-dependent absorption). **Timing (confirmed 2026-07-15): the athlete takes it at NIGHT with DINNER — his fattiest meal (steak + oil + olives). Do NOT prescribe/log iso "with breakfast"** — that was a repeated `/plan` miscue; dinner is both his routine and the correct fat vehicle. If the daily substitution was self-made, athlete to confirm with derm. Cumulative dose stays far below the ~120–150 mg/kg "durable-remission" target, so this is **ongoing control/suppression, not a cumulative-dose cure** — issue may return if stopped. Course length/target duration still TBC. Confirm dx is acne vulgaris vs fungal (malassezia) folliculitis (latter isn't first-line).

**Coaching implication — LARGELY DE-RISKED at this dose.** Side effects are dose-dependent; at ~3.3 mg/day the concerns below shrink to minor:
- **Musculoskeletal/CK/tendon → mostly off the table.** Unlikely to dent 35–42 km/wk + heavy lifting or the **Oct 25 sub-47 10K peak**. The earlier "start now vs after race" decision largely dissolves — can likely **start now** without compromising the peak. Still: don't auto-blame drug for fatigue, but don't expect material impact either.
- **Bloods:** baseline lipids/LFTs still prudent but expect minimal change; light-touch monitoring. Alcohol days still lipid/liver-relevant.
- **Skin/environment:** main nuisance is dry lips; mild dryness; photosensitivity still real for an outdoor summer runner (SPF — already doing); impaired healing minor but **picking/extraction still must STOP**.
- **Vitamin A additive** — glance at `supplements.md` multivitamin (low stakes here).
- On **finasteride** (no hard contraindication; minimal mood concern at microdose).

**Open question to put to derm:** target duration + what happens on stopping (control vs cure) — that's the real answer to the athlete's "will it ever go away."

**How to apply:** flag in `/report` skin block as a major regimen change; otherwise minimal `/plan` impact at this dose — don't over-attribute aches/fatigue to it. Re-read if dose is increased.
