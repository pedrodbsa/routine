Told by the athlete 2026-10-07, after the first Back + triceps session since Aug 13.

- **G1's "lat pulldown" is the dual pulley, loaded per side** (he loaded 50 kg on each pulley, logged 100 kg). It is not a stack lat-pulldown machine, so the logged number is the sum of two pulleys and "110 kg is the stack's top" is really 55 per side against a 60 kg/arm cap. Prescribe it as total and per side, and treat cross-session comparisons as approximate.
- **The "DB row" at G1 was a 16 kg kettlebell**, because G1 has kettlebells at 16 and 20 only. A prescribed 18 kg does not exist. Round row loads to 16 or 20, not 18.
- **He deliberately goes lighter on re-entry.** Last week's Chest + biceps re-entry session was too heavy and left him sore for days, so on Oct 7 he opened below the prescription (pulldown 100 not 105, row 16 not 18) and said he was weaker after the long break. Read a re-entry shortfall as intent, not regression.

**Why:** the re-entry rule ("one step below every logged load") produced a session heavy enough to cost days of soreness. His own judgment on the day was lighter and was the right call.

**How to apply:** open a re-entry session at a clearly lighter dose than "one step below" (about two steps, or RPE 6), prescribe equipment that exists at G1, and do not flag a lower-than-prescribed load on a re-entry day as a miss. See [[feedback-verify-load-baseline-against-athlete]] for the same trust-the-athlete-on-loads principle.
