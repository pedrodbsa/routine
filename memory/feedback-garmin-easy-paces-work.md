The athlete reports that **when he followed Garmin's suggested paces exactly, his race predictions improved** (stated 2026-06-30). Garmin's daily base pace for him is ~6:20/km, which it (correctly) reads as a zone-2 effort — well under his 142 easy ceiling / 6:00 individual-threshold pace.

**Why:** Garmin's DSW engine is a polarized/80-20 model — lots of genuinely easy base, quality on its own cadence. Following it = he ran his easy days *actually easy* instead of letting them drift toward moderate, which is the discipline that builds the aerobic base and drove his prediction gains. The lesson is "run easy days truly easy," not "obey the watch every day."

**How to apply:**
- On easy/base days, prescribe genuinely easy Garmin-base pace (~6:20/km, HR well under 142) and say so — do not let easy runs creep up.
- When the DSW reads **Base** but markers/spacing justify a quality, you may override *up* (see the 2026-06-30 plan), but honor the easy:hard ratio by keeping every *other* run that week genuinely easy. Don't stack a second full quality just because markers are green — the protocol's 2-quality/week is a deliberate motivational/specificity tradeoff, and this athlete's data says the polarized distribution works for him.
- Weigh his DSW as a real input, not a formality (it caught the over-aggressive direction here). Relates to [[feedback-quality-session-periodization]] and [[feedback-pace-hr-calibration]].
