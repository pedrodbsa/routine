The athlete reports that **when he followed Garmin's suggested base paces exactly, his race predictions improved** (stated 2026-06-30). Following them meant his easy days were actually easy instead of drifting toward moderate.

**Why:** the gain came from a polarized distribution — genuinely easy base, quality on its own cadence. The lesson is "run easy days truly easy", not "obey the watch".

**How to apply:**
- Easy days sit in the functional band ~135–142 at the pace that produces (currently ~6:30–6:40/km outdoors, ~138 bpm). Do not let easy runs creep up.
- The week carries one full quality; strides and a planned progression finish are the second stimulus. Never stack a second full quality because markers are green.
- The Daily Suggested Workout is de-emphasized (`plan.md`, athlete call 2026-07-23): glance at it only when it diverges (a rest flag or a jump to a hard session). Relates to [[feedback-quality-session-periodization]] and [[feedback-pace-hr-calibration]].
