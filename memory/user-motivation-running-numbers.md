Asked at the 2026-09-29 consult what actually gets him out the door on a low day — body change, running numbers, a plan already written, or training with others — the athlete picked **"running numbers improving."**

**Why it matters:** the objective is now body composition first ([[project-objective-athletic-summer-2027]]), so a stack that scores only weight and waist would remove the one feedback loop that moves him. September's collapse (Sep 21–27) coincided with the prediction being stuck at 51:00 and the control run dark for nine weeks.

**How to apply:**
- Every `/report` carries the running numbers line regardless of phase: the Garmin 10K prediction against its milestone (≤50:30 Oct 18 · ≤49:30 Dec 1 · ≤48:30 Apr 1 — a motivation line, never a governor), the fortnightly treadmill control-run avg HR, and long-run pace-at-HR on matched routes.
- Name a gain when there is one (e.g. "6:33 @ 138 vs 6:39 @ 138, +1.5%"); do not bury it under the body-comp verdict.
- The control run is his cleanest number and it depends on a day that kept vanishing — protect the Monday slot.
- In the January–April cut, expect the prediction to lag the sessions; say so in advance so a flat predictor does not read as "the plan is failing."
