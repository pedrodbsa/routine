The athlete finds lowering food intake hard and would rather add exercise (stated 2026-06-18, asking whether to "up the cardio"). He is also time-constrained (work + toddler) and will not reliably add walks (2026-06-23).

**Why:** a deeper plan-day cut works against both his preference and his otherwise exact plan-day adherence ([[feedback-tracking-adherence]]), and extra hard running in a deficit costs recovery the quality sessions need.

**How to apply:**
- The deficit comes from the **plan-day tiers plus social-day control**. NEAT (steps, walks) is opportunistic extra: never a counted lever, never an adherence target, never prescribed to offset a skipped session ([[feedback-adjust-day-on-skipped-session]]).
- Do not answer "up the cardio" with more hard running.
- Plan-day meals are the last lever, not the first. If the scale will not move on weeks with controlled social days, say plainly that a plan-day trim has to come back.
