The Dec 12 10K (São Silvestre Coimbra, 18:30, B-effort run hard with friends) has +119 m / −119 m over 10.02 km, about 12 m/km, against Condeixa's ~+45 m. The 2025 course was +147 m. The athlete asked on 2026-10-26 whether to train the climbing and said "I'm totally fine with doing treadmill work. I want to be in good shape."

**Why:** he trains at Choupal, which is flat, and knows no quiet hills in Coimbra. The rolling profile is worth roughly 15–30 s at equal effort (coach estimate, not measured), so it justifies adjusting sessions, not adding a block.

**How to apply:**
- **Hill strides:** six 15 s at 6–8% treadmill incline, walk back at 0%, after an easy run, every other week from Nov 16 (Nov 16 and Nov 30). They replace that week's flat strides. None in the Dec 7–13 race week.
- **Threshold:** the Nov 9–22 continuous session (2 × 12 → 25 min at 165–172) runs on the treadmill with incline changes (3 min at 1%, 2 min at 3–4%), pace floating, HR governing. Dec 7 10K-pace reps stay flat and are HR-governed.
- **Long runs:** on a saved rolling Garmin course on the non-progression weeks (Coimbra 13.28 km Jan 31 +117 m, Coimbra 16.45 km Jan 18 +88 m, S. Silvestre 2025 +147 m), cap 142, walk the steep bits. Ask which course is near him before choosing.
- No long downhill repeats, for leg size only ([[feedback-run-over-legs-legs-bulky]]); soreness is not a constraint for him ([[user-muscle-soreness-rarely-limiting]]).
- Race day keeps the Oct 25 shape (slow km 1, close from km 8) and is judged by HR and effort, not splits.
- The protocol side (running.md strides slot and Race Execution for Dec 12) is proposed through `/report`, never edited from a daily plan.
