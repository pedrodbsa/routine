# Planning Coherence Rules

## Training & Nutrition Timing Must Be Consistent

When creating daily plans, workout timing and meal timing must be logically coherent:

1. **Post-workout nutrition follows the workout** - Never schedule "post-workout shake" before the actual workout
2. **Meal timing adapts to workout timing** - If workout is at 14:00, post-workout nutrition is ~14:30-15:00
3. **Check the logic** - Before finalizing any plan, verify that nutrition timing makes sense relative to workout timing

### Example of WRONG planning:
- 12:30 Post-workout shake
- 14:00 Workout
→ This is incoherent - shake is before workout

### Example of CORRECT planning:
- 14:00 Workout  
- 14:30 Post-workout shake
→ Nutrition follows training

## General Rule
Always determine workout time first, then build nutrition timing around it.
