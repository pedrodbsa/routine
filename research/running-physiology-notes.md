# Running Physiology Notes

> Educational reference moved from protocols/running.md. Not needed for daily plan generation.

## Post-Smoking Recovery Context

Even 1 year smoke-free, cardiovascular adaptations continue:
- Stroke volume optimization takes 2-3 years of consistent aerobic training
- Heart compensates for lower stroke volume by beating more frequently
- Lung capillary density and oxygen transfer improve gradually over 1-5 years

Result: Similar race times achieved through raw effort (higher HR) rather than efficiency.

## Lactate: Fuel, Not Waste

- At any pace, the body produces and clears lactate simultaneously
- The "threshold" is where production equals clearance (~4.0 mmol/L)
- Training at 2.3-3.0 mmol/L upregulates MCT1/MCT4 transporters
- Norwegian model: 2x more quality volume at sub-threshold vs traditional tempo

## Energy Systems

| System     | Duration  | Primary Fuel  | Training Method                |
| ---------- | --------- | ------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Alactic    | 0-10 sec  | ATP-CP        | Hill sprints, strides          |
| Glycolytic | 10-90 sec | Glucose       | Short repeats, VO2max work     |
| Aerobic    | >90 sec   | Fat + Glucose | Easy runs, threshold intervals |

Half marathon is 95%+ aerobic.

## Muscle Fiber Recruitment

- Type I (slow-twitch): Primary endurance fibers, trained by easy volume
- Type IIA (fast-twitch oxidative): Recruited as Type I fatigue, trained by long runs and threshold work
- Type IIB (fast-twitch glycolytic): Speed/power, trained by hills and sprints

Long runs (90+ min) recruit Type II fibers for endurance work after Type I glycogen depletion.

## Methodology Selection Rationale

| Methodology | What We Take | Why |
| ----------- | ------------ | --- |
| MAF | Strict aerobic ceiling, monthly pace testing | Rebuilds post-smoking aerobic base |
| Polarized | 80/20 intensity discipline | Prevents chronic fatigue |
| Norwegian | Sub-threshold intervals, 3 quality/week | More threshold volume with less fatigue |
| Lydiard | Sequential phases | Structured periodization |
| Daniels | VDOT pace precision | Objective targets |

## MAF Test Interpretation

| Pattern | Meaning | Action |
| ------- | ------- | ------ |
| Pace improving | Aerobic system developing | Continue |
| Plateau 3+ months | Base maximized | Add sub-threshold |
| Pace declining | Overtraining/illness | Reduce volume |

Expected progression post-smoking: Months 1-3 rapid (10-20s/km/month), 4-6 moderate (5-10s), 7+ gradual.
