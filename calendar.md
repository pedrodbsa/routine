# CALENDAR - MASTER

> This file holds the stack's dates: races, phase boundaries, checkpoints, deadlines, appointments,
> reminders and TODOs. It owns **when**. `protocols/current-status.md` owns **what and why** (targets,
> priorities, the reasoning), and `/report` step 9 checks that the two agree.
>
> `/plan` reads today's items, overdue items and the next 7 days. `/recap` reads tomorrow's.
> `/report` reads the next 4 weeks and moves past items to `## Done`. `/consult` writes every
> approved date here. The athlete adds reminders and TODOs from Telegram through `/log`.
>
> **Kind** is one of race · phase · checkpoint · deadline · appointment · reminder · todo.
> **Owner** is athlete or coach.
> Seeded 2026-09-29 from the consult record (`logbook/2026-09/consult-2026-09-29.md`).
