## Phase 1: Post-HM Recovery (Mar 30 – Apr 12)

### Purpose
Recover from race, establish tracking habits, set body comp baselines.

### Running
- **Week 1:** Complete rest Mon-Wed, then 3 easy runs 4-5 km each (<148 outdoor). ~15 km total.
- **Week 2:** 4 easy runs, 5-7 km each. ~25 km total. No quality work.

### Strength
- **Week 1:** 1 light full-body session (mid-week, RPE 5-6). Reintroduce movement.
- **Week 2:** Resume 3x/week (push/pull/legs). Hypertrophy rep ranges (3-4x8-12). Start at 80% of previous weights.

### Nutrition
- **Maintenance: 2,300 cal.** 160g protein, 230g carbs, 70g fat.
- No deficit. Post-race recovery. Refill glycogen, restore hormones.
- **Habit focus:** Track every meal in Cronometer for 14 straight days.

### Monitoring
- **Daily morning weigh-in** on Withings (post-bathroom, pre-food) from Day 1. 7-day rolling average is the metric.
- 3 key checkpoints: Day 1 (Mar 30), Day 7 (Apr 5), Day 14 (Apr 12) — establish post-race baseline before cutting begins.

Primary fat loss phase. 10K-specific speed development. Double-day sessions.

| Sat | Easy 5-6 km | Optional arms/shoulders (30 min) | Double day |
| Sun | Long run 14-16 km | -- | Fueled as quality day (see nutrition) |

**Quality rotation (2x/week):**

| Week | Session 1 (Tue) | Session 2 (Fri) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Norwegian long: 3x10 min sub-T | 10K-pace: 6x1km |
| 2 | Cruise intervals: 5x1km sub-T | Tempo: 25 min continuous |
| 3 | Norwegian short: 8x3 min sub-T | 10K-pace: 8x1km |
| 4 (Jun 16) | Deload -- 1 easy tempo 20 min | Rest |
| 5 | Norwegian long: 3x12 min sub-T | 10K-pace: 5x1.5km |
| 6 | Tempo: 30 min continuous | VO2max: 5x800m |

- Week 4 deload + diet break (maintenance 2,300) aligns with June 10K race week (Jun 15-20). Race on **Sat Jun 20** (note: June 20 is a Saturday, not Sunday — verify with race organizer).
- Long runs capped at 16 km (10K training, not HM).
- **Day-type frequency (typical week):** 1 rest day (Thu — always), 3 easy-run days (Mon/Wed/Sat), 2 quality days (Tue/Fri), 1 long-run day (Sun).
- **Thursday is always rest from running.** This is carried forward from `training.md` — PT legs + no running. Quality days on Tue/Fri are buffered: Tuesday quality is preceded by an easy day (Mon) and followed by an easy day (Wed). Friday quality is preceded by Thursday rest (legs, no running) and followed by an easy day (Sat). Thursday legs and Friday quality are adjacent, but Thursday has no running — the legs-only session provides adequate separation from Friday's running quality.

- Push/Pull/Legs + optional Sat arms/shoulders (30 min pump).
- 4x10-12 compounds, 3x12-15 accessories. 16-18 sets/session.
- Drop 4th session if fatigued (BB <60, lingering soreness).

## Non-Negotiables (Across All Phases)

### Nutrition
- Track every meal in Cronometer. Daily. No exceptions.
- Protein floor: 160-165g/day depending on phase.
- Pre-quality-run carbs mandatory (banana + coffee 30 min before).
- Fasted easy runs OK (<60 min).
- Eating window: 12:30-21:00 (adjust if adherence suffers).

### Strength
- 3x/week minimum during all cut phases. This is the muscle-preservation signal.
- Log every weight, every session. No logging = no progression.
- Progressive overload via double progression (reps first, then weight).

### Monitoring
- **Daily morning weigh-in** on Withings (post-bathroom, pre-food). This is non-negotiable during a multi-month cut — weekly-only data is too noisy with refeeds, races, and water fluctuations.
- **7-day rolling average** is the decision metric, not individual daily readings. Compare weekly averages, not day-to-day.
- **Full body comp scan (BF%, lean mass, bone mass)** weekly on Sunday AM — the Withings does this automatically with the daily weigh-in, but Sunday's reading is the one used for trend tracking.
- Daily files with plan + log (meals checked off, weights logged).
- Recovery markers: HRV, BB, RHR, stress (Garmin daily).
- **ACWR computed weekly** (acute km / 4-week rolling avg km). Sweet spot: 0.8-1.3. See `protocols/running.md` for thresholds.

### Adjustment Triggers

| Signal | Action |
|---|---|
| Weight stalls 2 consecutive weeks | Audit tracking. If honest, drop rest-day cals -100. |
| Weight drops >0.8 kg/week | Add 100 cal (carbs). |
| Strength drops >10% for 2 sessions | Raise easy-day cals +100. |
| Easy runs >155 bpm at normal pace | Raise training-day carbs +30g. |
| Energy crashes mid-run | Add 50g carbs around training. |
| Lean mass declining on Withings | Raise cals 100-200 (carbs first). |
| 3+ untracked days in a row | Reassess deficit depth, not willpower. |
| Sleep <6h for 3+ nights | Drop to 1 quality run/week until resolved. |
| RHR elevated >5 bpm for 3+ days | Deload week regardless of schedule. |
| HRV 7-day avg drops >15% from baseline for 1+ week | Reduce quality to 1x/week, assess sleep and deficit depth. |
| BB consistently <30 at wake for 3+ days | Rest day + extra sleep. If persists, deload week. |
| Fewer than 2 strength sessions for 2 consecutive weeks | Mandatory scheduling with reduced volume (2x20 min full-body) to maintain habit. |
| Low motivation / dreading training for 5+ days | Insert 1-week diet break at maintenance + fun runs only. |

## Key Risks

1. **Adherence to tracking.** Self-identified as the #1 issue. The plan is only as good as the execution. First 2 weeks of tracking are habit-building — if they fail, the deficit is fiction.
2. **Skipping strength.** Also self-identified. Muscle loss accelerates in a deficit without resistance training. The metabolism drop on Withings confirms this is already happening.
3. **Deficit fatigue at weeks 14-18.** Cut Block 2 is long. Diet breaks and refeeds are built in, but mental discipline matters.
4. **Trail 21K recovery.** If the trail race is harder than expected (elevation, terrain), Cut Block 1 start may need an extra easy week.
5. **October 10K weather/conditions.** Race performance depends on factors outside training control.
6. **Body fat target realism.** 23% BF by August is the realistic target with good adherence. 21% is the optimistic end and requires near-perfect tracking and zero missed strength sessions. Set expectations at 22-23%.
